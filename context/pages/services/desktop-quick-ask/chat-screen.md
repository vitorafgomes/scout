## Chat screen

Since 2026-11-07 the Chat screen follows `context/prototypes/desktop/Main.dc.html`, and since 2026-11-10 in each state the artboard draws ([[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]], version 1): no title row of its own, since the top bar names the screen, with "Chat" a heading for a screen reader only (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:111`); Clear, a Material outlined button, and Read answers aloud are the screen's top-bar actions, a template the shell's top bar draws after the title (`:72`); a column of at most 880 px from the left edge of the page, with 24 px padding and 16 px between turns (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:397`), the owner's question as the accent bubble on the right (`:476`, 10 by 14 px padding, at most 520 px), with "You" and the time kept for a screen reader only (`data-test="who-user"`, `:88`), Scout's reply as a card on the left (`data-test="scout-reply"`, `:122`, `--sc-surface` with a `--sc-line` border, radius 14, padding 16, `:481`) that holds the header ("Scout" at 14 px, the time, and the read-aloud control at its right, no mark, `data-test="who-scout"`, `:123`), the reply, the findings and verdict cards and the citations; the composer as the artboard's bare row with no card around it (`:540`), the field 40 px tall on `--sc-bg` with radius 10, the talk button centred on it, Send an accent icon button named Send (`:346`), Stop a white square on `--sc-danger`, and the keys line 16 px under the row (`data-test="composer-hint"`, `:366`); a turn Scout is still answering sits below the approval strip, not in the conversation: the question, the text so far with no card, and a bare line saying what Scout is doing, in a region named "Scout is answering" that is announced politely (`data-test="running-turn"`), and it joins the conversation as a card once it ends. That region is the screen's only live one: the conversation list is not, so a turn that streamed is read once; a turn that joins the list without passing through it, a notice that arrives done or a question refused because Scout is not running, is read once from a polite status line (`data-test="announce"`), and so is the end of a running turn that failed or was stopped, by what its card shows under the reply (the failure sentence, or Interrupted); the history the screen opens on is not read. Focus on the read-aloud control or a link of a running turn moves to the same control in its card when the turn ends, and only then: focus elsewhere, such as the composer, stays where it is; and an empty conversation shows the Scout mark, "Ask Scout anything, or hold the microphone button to talk." and the suggestion "Try: list my open pull requests", which only fills the box (`data-test="empty"`, `:185`). Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.spec.ts` ("the chat as in the prototype": the bubble and the card in the light and dark theme, the composer, the empty state; "Chat as Main.dc.html draws it, state by state": the title, Clear and Read answers aloud, the owner's turn, the reply header for each reply state, the running turn, Stop, the composer, and the artboard's Answer, Conversation and Voice conversation states).

The Chat screen gets one answer at a time, as the quick-ask window does: since 2026-11-09 its field, Send and Enter stay on while a plain turn runs and a question sent then stops that turn and is asked (main's `stopRunning`; `send`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:820`), Clear is on while Scout answers and stops the answer (`:79`), and a Clear main refuses shows the reason on the error line (`clear`, `:836`); while a card's issues are being queued Send and Clear are off with "Queuing cards; cannot be stopped." and main refuses with `local/busy`; since 2026-11-07 its question box starts at one row and grows with the text up to six rows, Stop is disabled while a card post runs ("Queuing cards; cannot be stopped."), and its composer holds the microphone inside the question field on its right, with Send, and Stop before it while Scout answers, beside the field (`data-test="field"`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:286`), and a turn stopped after part of its reply keeps that reply with "Interrupted" under it (`data-test="failure"`, `:143`). The main window never scrolls as a page: the post-connect shell's host is fixed to the window (`position: fixed; inset: 0`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/post-connect-shell.component.ts:132`) and its grid fills it (`:141`), and since 2026-11-09 the page has no margin (`ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss:168`), so no second scrollbar shows beside the main area's (until then the wizard frame behind the shell, 100vh tall inside the body's 8 px margin, made the document 16 px taller than the window); until 2026-11-07 the host and the grid were `height: 100vh`.

- **Chat screen.** The main window's Chat screen (route `chat`, first in the sidebar) asks Scout over the same `scout:local.*` channels, for owners whose desktop does not deliver the global shortcut or show the tray (COSMIC on Wayland). It and the quick-ask window show one and the same conversation, held in main.
  - `LocalConversation` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/local-conversation.ts:15`) is provided in root in each window; it loads the list with `scout:local.getTurns` and follows `scout:local.turns`, so a reply that finishes while another screen is open, or a question asked from the quick-ask window, still lands in the history. The history is in memory only, never on disk, and Clear empties it for both windows (`:91`).
  - Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line. One answer runs at a time: the microphone waits, a new question or Clear stops the running answer, and Stop sends `scout:local.cancel` for the running turn (`:51`).
  - Since 2026-11-07 the screen is busy from the moment its ask leaves for main, typed or spoken, so the microphone stays off and Stop shows while the question is on its way (`sending`, `busy`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:676`); a refusal keeps the typed question in the box, which empties only once main took the ask and only if the owner did not change it meanwhile (`send`, `:827`, `:832`). With no AI Source connected the question field ("Connect an AI Source first"), the microphone and Send are off and nothing is sent, read from the same `LlmConfigStore` the quick-ask window uses, which the screen loads as it opens (`noSource`, `:678`, `:292`). The list follows a streaming reply to the bottom unless the reader scrolled more than 48 px up, as the quick-ask window does (`:717`, `onTurnsScroll`, `:806`). Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.spec.ts` ("B1 Chat keeps the typed question when main refuses it", "B5 with no AI Source connected, Chat keeps the field, the microphone and Send off and sends nothing", "B5 while a question is still on its way to main, Chat keeps the microphone off and does not start recording", "B7 Chat follows a streaming reply to the bottom of the conversation").
  - Since 2026-11-07 the question box starts at one row and grows with the text up to six rows (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:561`, `:561`), Stop is disabled while a card post runs (`:334`), and the microphone sits inside the question field on its right, with Send, and Stop before it while Scout answers, beside the field (`data-test="field"`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:286`); a turn stopped after part of its reply keeps it, with "Interrupted" under it (`:143`).
  - While main reports that a live listen is open for another window, such as a meeting in the quick-ask window, the microphone and the talk button are off; the screen's own hold and voice conversation never turn them off (`listeningElsewhere`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:684`, read from `scout:local.listening` and `scout:local.listeningChanged`, `:734`).
  - Holding the microphone button dictates as in the quick-ask window: the heard text fills the message box, release sends the last final as a question marked `voice` with a mic in the bubble (`data-test="question-voice"`), and nothing heard says "No speech heard; nothing was sent." (`startRecording`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:866`; `stopRecording`, `:881`). Losing focus or leaving the screen throws what was heard away.
  - With no running worker the turn's reply is "Scout is not running. Start it from the tray or the app.", and for that `local/not-running` refusal only the turn also shows Start Scout (`:53`), which calls `scout:worker.start` once and then disappears (`:255`); the owner asks again with Send. Any other refusal shows on the error line.
  - Clear, on the Chat screen and in the quick-ask window, calls `scout:local.clearTurns` through `LocalConversation.clear`, and that one request is also how the worker's memory for the local channel is reset and its running answer stopped: main cancels a running turn, empties the conversation it holds and writes `{"type":"reset"}` to the worker (gap plan G6, main's and the local adapter's side of the same change, [[pages/services/adapter-local]]); the renderer adds no request of its own. With `context` (above) the transcript, the screen text and the last answer stay out of the stored turn.

- **Live listening, answers and the worker restart.** Main tells its one restart path when the first live listen opens and when the last one ends (`onListeningChanged`, from `pushListening`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1444`; `isListening`, `:1818`), and since 2026-11-08 when a turn starts running and when the last running turn ends (`onAnsweringChanged`, from `broadcast`, `:768`, which every change of the conversation passes through; `isAnswering`, `:1819`): a session ends on the worker's `complete` or `fail`, never on the stop request, so a restart waiting for the listen never cuts the trailing final, and a restart waiting for an answer runs once that answer completes, fails or is cancelled ([[pages/services/desktop-ui]], Worker restarts). Test: "main hears the first listen start and the last one end only when the worker completes it, ..." in `ui/packages/main/test/quick-ask.test.ts`.
