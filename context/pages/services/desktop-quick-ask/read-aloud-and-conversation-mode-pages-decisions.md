## Read aloud and conversation mode

[[pages/decisions/0050-read-aloud-and-conversation-mode]], built 2026-11-07, in the quick-ask window and on the Chat screen; the worker's voice is on [[pages/services/adapter-local]] (Read aloud) and [[pages/services/operator]] (Reply speech).

- **The channel.** `scout:local.speak` with `{ text, sample? }`, 1 to 2000 characters, goes to the worker as `{"type":"speak","id":"v-<n>","text","providerVoice"?,"localVoice"?}` and answers `{ contentType, base64 }` from the worker's `audio` line, or rejects with the worker's `fail` sentence as `local/speak-failed` (`registerSpeech`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/speech.ts:114`, `:214`, routed from `handleWorkerLine` at `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1848`). The voice is the one saved in Models, Speech: main reads the vault keys `speech.voice.provider` and `speech.voice.local` for every sentence (`savedVoice`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/speech.ts:119`), so a new choice reaches the next sentence with no worker restart, in read aloud and in conversation mode alike; a saved value that is not a voice id (letters, digits, `_ : . # -`, at most 80) is left out, and a vault that cannot be read sends the sentence in the default voice and logs why. A `sample` of `{ provider }` or `{ local }` sends that voice instead, a local one with `"localOnly":true` (`sampleVoice`, `:72`); any other sample is refused before the worker with "A sample names one voice: an AI Source voice or a local voice id.". `scout:local.voices` sends `{"type":"voices","id":"vl-<n>"}` and answers `{ provider, providerVoices, localVoices, defaultLocal }` from the worker's `voices` line (`:189`), with its own 10 s timeout ("Scout did not list its voices in time."), "Scout stopped before it listed its voices." on exit, and "Scout could not list its voices; see Scout logs." for a blank fail or a malformed list. The worker checks the voice and refuses one it does not have ([[pages/services/adapter-local]], Read aloud). No answer in 60 s (`SPEAK_TIMEOUT_MS`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/speech.ts:16`) is "Scout did not read that aloud in time.", a worker that exits or a dispose "Scout stopped before it read that aloud.", no worker "Scout is not running. Start it from the tray or the app."; the sentence is never logged, only its id and length. `buildEnv` always sets `SCOUT__SPEECH__DIRECTORY` to `<userData>/speech`, empty without the folder (`ui/packages/main/src/worker/build-env.ts:129`), and the supervisor never passes an inherited value of it in any spelling (`ui/packages/main/src/worker/supervisor.ts:390`).

- **Read aloud.** Each reply with text has a speaker (`ReadAloudButtonComponent`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/read-aloud-button.component.ts:53`; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:687`, in the quick-ask window at the right of the reply's header since 2026-11-10, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:131`), "Read this answer aloud", pressed and "Stop reading aloud" while it reads. "Read answers aloud" is an icon toggle (`ReadAloudSwitchComponent`: the `speaker` glyph when on, `speaker-off` when off, named and tooltipped "Read answers aloud" with `aria-pressed`, no text label, in the quick-ask window the same 32 px button as the dictation microphone, in the tools tray after the Translate select since 2026-11-10 (it was under the composer), the tray opening when the voice fails so the reason is in sight and the tools button still closing it, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:420`, `:1580`, with the reason the voice failed as an alert under it; on the Chat screen a 40 px round button with a `--sc-line` border in the top bar, after Clear, on `--sc-surface-2` while pressed, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:84`, with the reason the voice failed as an alert over the composer, `data-test="voice-error"`); on, it reads every reply to a question this window asked as it streams, and is kept as `on` or `off` under `scout:read-aloud` in renderer storage, read and written behind try/catch (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/read-aloud.ts:39`, `:95`). `ReadAloud` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/read-aloud.ts:54`) splits the reply into sentences with `SentenceStream` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/sentences.ts:66`): fenced code blocks and table rows are left out, a link is read by its label and a bare address not at all (`:1`, `:3`, `:38`), a list marker is never read, also while a numbered item is still streaming, a piece over 300 characters is cut after its last `,`, `;` or `:`, else at its last space, else at 300 (`MAX_PIECE_CHARS`, `:10`), and a sentence is given once it ends with a stop and a space or a line break. `SpeechQueue` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/speech-queue.ts:16`) makes a sentence once the one before it starts playing, so at most one is made ahead of the one playing, and plays them in order through Web Audio, decoded in memory with no media URL (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-player.ts:28`); a sentence the voice could not make, or one Web Audio could not play (the context would not resume, or the audio would not decode), stops the reading and the switch shows the reason under it, such as the no-voice one; a playback that fails after a stop says nothing (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/speech-queue.ts:81`). The speaker again, or Esc, stops at once; in the quick-ask window the Esc that stops a reading or a conversation does not close the window (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1571`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:74`).

- **Conversation mode.** A round 32 px button, white on `--sc-accent-strong`, with the `sound-wave` glyph sits beside the dictation microphone (`TalkButtonComponent`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/talk-button.component.ts:120`; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:819`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:324`), named "Start a voice conversation" or "End the voice conversation" with `aria-pressed` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/talk-button.component.ts:41`), off while a held recording has the microphone or no AI Source is connected, and in the quick-ask window also during a live listen, and always clickable while on; on the Chat screen and, since 2026-11-10, in the quick-ask window, off for want of an AI Source, its title, which a screen reader reads as its description, is "Connect an AI Source first" (`offReason`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/talk-button.component.ts:125`; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:326`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:821`). On, it writes Listening, Thinking or Speaking beside it, and its wave moves gently while listening or speaking unless the system asks for reduced motion (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/talk-button.component.ts:126`, `:133`). While Scout listens, the partial text of the line being spoken follows the state, in its own `aria-live="polite"` span (`data-test="talk-heard"`, `:52`; `heard`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/conversation-mode.ts:96`). `ConversationMode` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/conversation-mode.ts:75`) opens its own listen on lane `me` and its own microphone, the one chosen in Settings, Audio, with automatic gain off and echo cancellation and noise suppression on (`CONVERSATION_MICROPHONE_CONSTRAINTS`, `:36`, opened through `AudioDevices.open`, see Microphone and speaker below), so the room keeps one level between turns; each final line from that listen, heard after the transcriber's pause, is sent as an ordinary question (`onHeard`, `:238`) and its reply is read aloud, then it listens again: listening, thinking, speaking, listening. From the moment a line is heard until the reply has been read every chunk goes out as silence (`:99`, `:232`), so Scout never hears itself. A click or Esc ends it from any state: the voice stops, the microphone closes and the listen ends. A click while Scout listens also follows that listen until main ends it, at most 10 s, and asks the final lines heard by then, joined, as one question; with the conversation off, its answer is read aloud only under the switch (`end`, `:190`; `finish`, `:208`). Esc, and closing the window or the Chat screen, drop what was in flight, also after a click while that listen drains, so neither its end nor the 10 s bound asks it (`escape`, `:182`); turning the conversation on again while the last listen drains drops that listen's line. A listen that ends under the conversation, such as one another listen took, or a chunk main refuses, turns it off with the reason on the button: the event's `error`, "Scout stopped listening." when it has none, or main's refusal (`failed`, `:224`); a refusal for a listen that already ended does not touch the next one. Interrupting Scout by speaking over it is not built.

- **Microphone and speaker.** Every capture opens the microphone chosen in Settings, Audio ([[pages/services/desktop-ui]], Settings), through one opener, `AudioDevices.open` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-devices.ts:121`): the hold-to-talk dictation and a meeting's own lane with `MICROPHONE_CONSTRAINTS` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/wav-recorder.ts:51`, the recorder's default source, `:91`), the conversation with its own constraints, automatic gain off (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/conversation-mode.ts:152`); the system audio lane is untouched. A chosen device adds `deviceId: { exact }` to the same constraints, System default adds nothing. The choice is read from renderer storage (`scout:audio-input`, `scout:audio-output`) on every open, so a choice made in the main window reaches the quick-ask window's next capture with no reload. A chosen microphone that is not connected (`OverconstrainedError` or `NotFoundError` on open) is opened again as the default one, and a `devicechange` that no longer lists it says the same; either way the notice "The chosen microphone is not connected; Scout uses the default one." shows once, until the device is listed again, and on open only once the default one has opened, so with no microphone at all the owner sees only "No microphone was found."; and the choice is kept, so a replugged device is used again. A refused microphone says "Scout was not allowed to use the microphone.", none at all "No microphone was found.", a busy one "The microphone is busy: another program is using it." (`microphoneError`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-devices.ts:42`), in the quick-ask error line, on the Chat screen's error line and on the conversation button alike (a hold on the Chat screen whose microphone did not open used to show nothing, since its release found no recording to finish; `startRecording` now shows the listen's reason, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:866`); any other failure keeps "Scout could not use the microphone.". Read aloud and the voice samples of Models, Speech play on the chosen speaker: before each sentence the player points its Web Audio context at it with `setSinkId` when it is not there yet (`route`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-devices.ts:140`; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-player.ts:24`), and a speaker that is not connected falls back to the default one with "The chosen speaker is not connected; Scout uses the default one." once, the sentence still playing.
