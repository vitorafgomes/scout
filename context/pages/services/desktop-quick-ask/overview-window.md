## Overview

- **Window.**
  - `openQuickAsk()` (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:257`) is what the shortcut calls, and what the tray's Ask Scout... item calls.
  - It shows one 440 by 700 frameless, always-on-top window (`QUICK_ASK_SIZE`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:117`, the artboard's size since 2026-11-10; 640 by 420 before), resizable down to 360 by 240, sandboxed, with context isolation and the existing preload. Navigation and new windows are blocked. Since 2026-11-07 the window reads as one column (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:138`): a slim header that is the drag strip, with Scout's mark, the name with the status stacked under it (`:152`) and its dot (`:155`), green with the AI Source and model in use (`source`, `:1492`), grey with "No AI Source connected" when the owner chose none (`noSource`, `:1500`), the accent colour and pulsing with "Answering…" while a turn runs, or "Queuing cards…" while the running turn posts a review or opens issues (`:162`) (still when the owner asks for reduced motion), the theme switch (Light, Dark, System, `data-test="theme-light"`, `theme-dark`, `theme-system`, `:165`), which asks main through `ThemeStore.set` as the main window's top bar does and shows main's answer as the pressed one (since 2026-11-10 the artboard's raised pill, `--sc-surface` in the light theme and `--sc-line` in the dark one under `--sc-fg`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:997`; until then the accent on the tint), the tools toggle (`data-test="tools-toggle"`, `:183`), Clear (`data-test="clear-conversation"`, `:207`, on while Scout answers a question since 2026-11-08, off while the window's own ask is on its way to main, while a card's issues are being queued, with the title "Queuing cards; cannot be stopped.", or when the list is empty) and Close (`:228`); the tools area (`data-test="tools"`, `:245`), which holds the tools tray (`data-test="tools-tray"`, `:251`, closed by default, open while the toggle is pressed and kept open while a lane listens or a meeting runs so its Stop stays in reach, `trayShown`, `:1480`) and every status line, block and panel (the meeting rows, the translation disclosure, Text from the screen, the error lines, the transcript); the turn list, the one scroll (`:591`), which with no turn shows the empty state (`:593`): the Scout mark, "Ask anything. Hold Space or the mic to talk." and one suggestion, "Try: list my open pull requests", which only fills the box (`:620`), or, with no AI Source, "Connect an AI Source in Scout, under AI Sources, so Scout can answer." and no action, because no channel lets this window open the main window's AI Sources; with no AI Source the question field is disabled and says "Connect an AI Source first" (`:780`, `:787`), and the microphone, Space push-to-talk and Send are off too (`:798`, `:844`, `:1654`), as are `ask` and `startRecording` themselves (`:1912`, `:1982`); and the composer at the bottom (`:738`): an attached screenshot as a chip with its remove button, the question field with the microphone inside it on the right (`data-test="field"`, `:771`), Send beside it, which turns into a red Stop while a turn runs (`:841`, `:827`), and a hint line, "Enter sends · Shift+Enter for a new line · Esc closes" (`:865`); Space push-to-talk is named in the microphone's title, "Hold to talk, or hold Space with the box empty". Every button in the header, the tray and the composer shows an icon, and an icon-only one carries its name in `aria-label`. Since 2026-11-10 the header of a reply that is no longer running shows the turn's time as `HH:mm` after Scout's name and before the speaker, as the artboard draws it (`data-test="reply-time"`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:684`); the time is `askedAt`, when the question was asked or a notice arrived, since a turn carries no time of its answer. Since the same change the window paints only with the `--sc-*` tokens, with no dark fallback (`:886`), and is no longer pinned dark by the global stylesheet, so it follows the light and dark theme main pushes; the shared turn styles moved to the tokens too, which keeps the Chat screen dark under its canvas ([[pages/services/desktop-ui]], Theme and notices). The question box is a textarea of one row that grows with the text up to five rows of 20 px and then scrolls (`field-sizing: content`, `max-height: 120px`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1218`; six rows until 2026-11-10), at most 4,000 characters; Enter sends and Shift+Enter inserts a line break (`onEnter`, `:1660`, bound to `keydown.enter`, which Angular fires only for Enter without a modifier). The header and the composer never shrink; the tools area takes at most 40% of the window and scrolls on its own (`:1061`), so at the 240 px minimum height with every block open the composer stays whole. There is no "open in the app" button: no channel lets the quick-ask page show the main window, and ADR 0028 adds none. Every input, button, select and textarea is marked `no-drag` so it stays clickable. The owner approved the layout on 2026-11-07 from a prototype ("go ahead with everything"); before it, since 2026-11-06, the page read grip, tools row, turns and a question line with Mic, Listen and Close.
  - Tests (2026-11-07, the artboard): `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.spec.ts` ("the header's theme switch: light, dark or system asks main for it through the theme store and is the one pressed", the matrix of an AI Source connected or none by Enter, Send, Space and the microphone, "the status line sits under the name", and the status matrix with a card posting).
  - The window loads `index.html` at `#/quick-ask`. When it is created it goes back to the bounds it was last left at, stored as JSON `{ x, y, width, height }` under the main-only vault key `quick-ask.window` whenever it hides or closes (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1062`), if they overlap the work area of a current display; otherwise, and when nothing or something malformed is stored, it goes to the floating button's corner of the chosen display (see Floating button). Since 2026-11-10 it never opens larger than the work area or past its edges (`fitOnDisplay`, `inCorner`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1071`, `:846`; `fitInWorkArea` and the clamped `placeInCorner`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/placement.ts:43`, `:18`): in a corner it is 440 by 700, or the work area's width or height where that is smaller, 16 px from the edges where they fit and at the edge otherwise; saved bounds, and a window only hidden when it shows again, keep their own size and are fitted to the work area of the display they overlap most, shrunk to it and moved the least distance into it, so a window dragged partly past an edge or with its top-left on a bar between work areas comes back where it was left; a window that overlaps no display, its monitor unplugged, opens in the corner of the chosen display. Every saved size is kept: a window saved at the 640 by 420 of the runs before that date reopens at that size until the owner resizes it, and a first run opens at 440 by 700. Tests: `ui/packages/main/test/quick-ask.test.ts`, "quick ask: the window opens at the 440 x 700 of its artboard, never larger than the work area and never off-screen"; `ui/packages/main/test/placement.test.ts`. A failed save is a warning. A window that was only hidden shows again where it was. It is focused, and hidden on blur or on `scout:local.hide`, which Escape and the page's Close button call (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1707`); hiding it shows the floating button again (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1085`). On the owner's COSMIC desktop (Wayland, Scout as an XWayland client) a window closed with Close came back behind other apps on the next click; the X window manager there (Smithay) never sets `_NET_WM_STATE_ABOVE` on Scout's windows and the X stacking list does not show native Wayland windows, so the exact cause is not visible from X11. The owner confirmed on 2026-11-02 that the window reopens in front after `open()` re-asserts it at the `floating` level and raises the window with `moveTop` every time it shows it, and `showBubble()` re-asserts it at `screen-saver` every time it shows the floating button, on every platform (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:986`, `:986`).

- **Channels.** The `scout:local.*` channels are listed with the rest of the desktop's `scout:*` channels on [[pages/services/desktop-channels]].
