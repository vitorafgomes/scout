## Overview

- **Approval cards.** Since 2026-11-07 an action that cannot be undone and waits for the owner shows in the chat and in the quick-ask window, so the owner approves it where the request came from and never has to open another menu ([[pages/decisions/0032-per-feature-write-permissions]], amendment 2026-11-07; the owner, translated: "if the person asks for a code review and it is approved by the bot, it must show in the chat for them to approve... The same goes for when it finds a bug"). The review findings card's own Approve and Request changes stay as they are ([[pages/decisions/0043-the-verdict-on-a-pull-request]] v1.1); these cards are for the actions the gate queued.

- **Which window asked.** `windowOf` (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:1815`) tells main whether an IPC call came from the quick-ask window or the floating button, so main's sender check reads it as `quick-ask` or `floating-button` ([[pages/decisions/0053-electron-fuses-and-ipc-sender-check]]): the quick-ask window may approve and reject the actions on its cards, the floating button may not, and neither may grant, enter credentials or take an operator decision (tests: the identity `ui/packages/main/test/quick-ask.test.ts:2192`, the refusals `ui/packages/main/test/ipc-features.test.ts:854`). `appPageCheck` (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:269`), which the microphone and screen-capture handlers use, is the check of Scout's own page that the sender check reuses.
  - **Placement.** `sc-approval-strip` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/approval-strip.component.ts:29`) sits right above the composer in both windows, on the Chat screen above the turn Scout is answering when there is one (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/chat/chat.component.ts:245`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:704`), headed "Waiting for your approval", newest request first, and stays until each card is decided; with only decided cards left it reads "Your decisions", and with none it is not drawn. It is a strip, not a card under the turn that caused it, because a queued action carries no turn: the worker's `RequestWriteRequest` and the control plane's `PendingAction` hold the feature, scope, target, summary and payload only, and tying one to a turn would thread the local adapter's turn id through `Scout.Llm`'s `IWriteGate`, the worker's `GrpcWriteGate`, `worker.proto`, `WriteGateService`, `IActionQueue` and its file, and `features.proto`.
  - **The card** (a `section` named by its `h3`): the feature, "Cannot be undone", "Requested <yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm>", the action in words read from its target or summary (`describeAction`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/pending-approvals.store.ts:96`): "Approve pull request #12 on acme/api", "Request changes on pull request #12 on acme/api", "Comment on pull request #12 on acme/api", "Comment on issue #9 on acme/api", "Post a review on pull request #4 on acme/api", "Open an issue on acme/api", else the action's own name; then "Target: <target>" and the summary. Reject (close icon, danger) shows "Reason (optional)" with Cancel and Reject, and moves focus to the reason; Approve (check icon, primary) is never focused by default and is disabled, with Reject, while that action's decision is in flight.
  - **The decision** goes through `scout:actions.approve` or `scout:actions.reject`, the Approvals screen's channels, with the trimmed reason when there is one (`PendingApprovals.decide`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/pending-approvals.store.ts:278`); a second click while the first is in flight sends nothing (`:283`). The card then shows its outcome: "Approved, running", "Done", "Rejected" or "Rejected: <reason>", "Failed: <reason>" read from the write log entry with that action's id, or "Already decided" when another window decided first; Dismiss takes a decided card off (`:251`). A refused decision shows its reason on the card as an alert and leaves Approve and Reject (Rule I). Since 2026-11-08 (manager default, the owner may override) an approval the control plane turned into a rejection, the action being asked before the feature's current write grant, keeps its card as "Rejected: asked before writes were granted; ask again" with no alert and no Approve, in the chat and the quick-ask window alike (`control-plane/action-rejected`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/pending-approvals.store.ts:305`; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/approval-strip.component.spec.ts`, "an approval refused as asked before the grant keeps its card as Rejected with that reason, not an error").
  - **One store per window** (`PendingApprovals`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/pending-approvals.store.ts`) reads `scout:actions.list` and keeps `pending` actions only; it reads again on `scout:actions.changed` (`:176`), when a chat turn ends, so an action queued during that turn shows at once (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/approval-strip.component.ts:351`), and every 15 s while a strip is shown (`:19`), which is how "Approved, running" reaches Done or Failed, since nothing pushes when the worker records the write. A list that cannot be read shows nothing new and keeps the chat as it was. A decision anywhere drops the action from every window's strip and from the Approvals count at once, and no window keeps an Approve for it ([[pages/services/desktop-ui]], Approvals).
  - Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/approval-strip.component.spec.ts` (action kind, verdict approve, verdict request changes, comment, issue, by status, pending, approved, executed, rejected, failed, in the chat and in the quick-ask window; approve once and the double click; disabled in flight; the focus; the region and heading; Reject with and without a reason and Cancel; a failed approve or reject; Dismiss; the reread at a turn's end and every 15 s; decided in the chat, the quick-ask window or Approvals, approve or reject, with no Approve left anywhere and the count at 1; two windows at once, the second "Already decided"; the two owner-reported bugs; the action in words; the theme tokens) and `ui/packages/main/test/control-plane-client.test.ts` (a second decision rejects as Already decided; an unreachable control plane does not).

- **Microphone.**
  - Holding Space while the question box is empty, or holding the microphone button, dictates (`onKeyDown`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1633`), never while a turn runs. Since 2026-11-07 (the owner: "when I speak it should show what I said being sent to Scout") hold to talk is a live listen on the `me` lane marked as a dictation (`startRecording`, `:1981`; `LiveListener.listen(lane, translate, dictation)`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/live-listener.ts`): the partials and finals fill the question box while held, and the line under the field reads "Listening. Release to send." with a pulsing mic in the error colour (`data-test="recording"` with `data-test="speaking"`, in place of the hint since 2026-11-10, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:860`), and its lines stay out of the transcript panel. Release (`stopRecording`, `:1995`) stops the lane and waits, at most 10 s, for the worker to end the session so the last final is in (`LiveListener.finish`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/live-listener.ts:195`), then sends the heard text as a typed question with `voice: true` through the same `ask` as typing; the turn keeps `voice` and the bubble shows a mic before the text (`data-test="asked-voice"`). Nothing heard sends nothing and says "No speech heard; nothing was sent." (`NO_SPEECH_HEARD`, `:21`); a session that ends with an error shows it on the error line and sends nothing. No turn reads "(voice message)" and, because the worker receives text, no reply on the desktop starts with the "> (voice)" quote; Discord and Slack keep sending audio, which the conductor transcribes and quotes ([[pages/services/conversation]]). The first press with no verified Whisper model on this computer opens "Download a voice model?" instead (see [[pages/services/desktop-ui]], Voice model on the Desktop card), and so do Listen, System audio and Meeting. Escape inside that dialog closes only the dialog (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1635`). Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/dictation.spec.ts` (the quick-ask microphone, Space and the Chat microphone × speech heard, no speech heard, transcription failed), `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/voice-model-ask.spec.ts`. Space starts push-to-talk only from the question box or from no field at all; in any other input, textarea, select or button (a card's editor, Text from the screen, the meeting title, the tools) Space does what that control does. Escape closes the window from the question box, from a button or from no field; inside another input, textarea or select it only leaves that field (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1643`, `:1638`).
  - `WavRecorder` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/wav-recorder.ts:71`) captures with `getUserMedia`, an `AudioContext` at 16 kHz and a `ScriptProcessorNode`, because the CSP blocks a blob-URL `AudioWorklet`. The processor reaches the output only through a gain node at 0 (`:156`), so the microphone is never played back through the speakers, where echo cancellation could take the owner's voice for echo.
  - For hold to talk it streams each chunk to the live transcriber and keeps no recording (until 2026-11-07 it kept the samples and sent a WAV on release). A stop that arrives while the microphone is still opening waits for it and then stops the stream and closes the context, and each start first releases any microphone still open. Escape, or the window losing focus, stops the lane, throws away what was heard and sends nothing. A microphone track that ends on its own while held (the device unplugged) stops the lane, sends nothing and shows "The microphone was disconnected." (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/live-listener.ts:163`), through `WavRecorder.start`'s `onEnded`, which a track's `ended` calls and the recorder's own stop never does (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/wav-recorder.ts:131`, cleared at `:169`); the Chat screen's hold to talk does the same (test "when the microphone track ends while the button is held, ..." in `chat.component.spec.ts`). Each time the window is shown or focused again the question box takes the focus (`onWindowFocus`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/quick-ask/quick-ask.component.ts:1678`).
  - `allowMicrophoneOnlyForApp` (`ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:284`, applied at `ui/packages/main/src/main.ts:1090`) grants `media` only to the app's own page (the `file:` URL of `index.html`, or the dev server origin) and only for audio. Other permissions keep Electron's default grant.
  - macOS gets `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` (`ui/build/electron-builder.yml:110`) and the audio-input entitlement (`ui/build/entitlements.mac.plist:11`).
