## How it works

A scene that shows the recording is rendered on its own (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:283`): `-ss <clip start> -t <clip length> -i <recording>`, the recording's picture scaled to fit and padded black to 1920x1080, `setsar=1` and 30 fps, the scene's text burned in as captions timed evenly across the clip, a fade in on the video's first scene only and a fade out over the last 0.4 s of its last scene only, `[0:a]aresample=48000,apad` for the recording's own sound and `-t <clip length>`, with no 2-second floor and no motion preset (`tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:471`, `:507`). The recording's length and write time stand in the segment's name like a picture's, so a replaced recording renders every segment again (`:526`), and a caption edit renders only its scene. A recording the media directory does not hold fails the assembly with "Scout could not find the recording in the project's media folder." before ffmpeg runs (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:119`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:545`), and a `ready` recording scene is never a placeholder (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:565`). The description lists the chapters at their scenes' clips: for a scene of the recording `PackageChecks` finds a chapter's first scene by the clip that starts where the chapter's section starts, not by its text, so a caption edit keeps every chapter (`src/Scout.Operator/PackageChecks.cs:262`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblyTests.cs:481`, `:498`). The video is one full 1080p30 re-encode, about 450 MB per 10 minutes at 6 Mbit/s, with no loudness normalisation.

**The animated character in the assembler.** `AssemblyStage` hands an `animated_character` project's `PresenterFile`, `MouthBox` and `PresenterSource` to the assembler (`src/Scout.Operator/AssemblyStage.cs:42`). Before the first segment the presenter picture's name goes through `MediaFiles.PathFor` (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:111`), and on the first presenter scene, once per assembly and never in an assembly without one, its size is read from the `Video:` line of `ffmpeg -i` (`:138`, `src/Scout.Operator/MediaProbe.cs:41`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:747`). Since phase 8c-B every scene of such a project that is not a clip of a recording is a presenter scene (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:121`), over its own backdrop: the scene's picture, else the project's background, else the dark card (`:123`). `PresenterArguments` (`:321`) builds it at 1920x1080 (`:76`): under `gentle` and `dynamic` the backdrop is scaled and padded to twice the frame and moved by the same `zoompan` as a picture scene (`:345`), under `still` it is looped at one frame a second, scaled and padded to the frame and turned to 30 fps (`:340`), and without one the dark card's `color` source runs at the frame size (`:354`). The presenter picture stands still over it, scaled to 45 percent of the frame's height, both sides even, in the lower right, 3 percent of the frame's width and height from its edges (`:70`, `:72`, `:359`, `:362`). A generated presenter, drawn on green, first goes through `chromakey=0x00FF00:0.1:0.2` (`:74`, `:433`); a built-in or the owner's picture is laid as it is. When the project has a mouth box, the scene has narration and lip sync succeeded, the mouth track is a third input, `-f concat -safe 0 -i mouth-track-<scene>.txt`, scaled to the mouth box in the placed picture's pixels, at least 2 by 2, and overlaid at its place. The captions move to the lower left, starting at the side margin and scaled with the frame (`:377`), then come the fades of a picture scene and the narration or silence as its sound. A 1024x1024 generated presenter with the mouth box (0.4, 0.6, 0.2, 0.1) over the scene's own picture under `gentle`, asserted argument by argument (`tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:587`):

```
-hide_banner -nostdin -y -i <media>/scene-s1.png -i <media>/presenter-<hex>.png
-f concat -safe 0 -i <media>/mouth-track-s1.txt -i <media>/scene-s1.wav
-filter_complex [0:v]scale=3840:2160:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=3840:2160:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2:color=black,setsar=1,
  zoompan=z=1+0.08*on/104:...:d=105:s=1920x1080:fps=30[bg];
  [1:v]chromakey=0x00FF00:0.1:0.2,scale=486:486,setsar=1[face];[bg][face]overlay=1376:562[body];
  [2:v]scale=97:49[mouth];[body][mouth]overlay=1570:854,
  drawtext=...:x=58:y=940:...,fade=t=in:st=0:d=0.4,fade=t=out:st=3.100:d=0.4,format=yuv420p[v];[3:a]aresample=48000,apad[a]
-map [v] -map [a] -t 3.500 <the render's video and audio encoding> <segment>
```

The same scene with a built-in presenter has the same placement, no `chromakey` and another segment name; at the preview's 1280x720 under `still` the picture is `scale=324:324` at `overlay=918:374` and the captions are 29 px at `x=38:y=627` (the same test).

`RhubarbLipSync` (`src/Scout.Operator/RhubarbLipSync.cs:23`) times the mouth with Rhubarb Lip Sync 1.14.0 (MIT), which Scout Desktop bundles and names as `Scout:Operator:RhubarbPath` ([[pages/services/desktop-operator]]), a separate program run through `IProcessRunner` as `rhubarb -f json -r phonetic --extendedShapes GHX -o <media>/mouth-cues-<scene>.json <narration>` with a timeout of 2 minutes plus 2 ms per millisecond of the narration's measured length (`:26`, `:118`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/RhubarbLipSyncTests.cs:27`, `:100`), on the phonetic recognizer, which needs no language model and so works in any language. A narration that is not WAV or OGG, the provider's MP3, is first turned into `lipsync-<scene>.wav`, 16 kHz mono, with ffmpeg through `MediaFiles.RenderAsync` in the media directory, and deleted when the run ends (`src/Scout.Operator/RhubarbLipSync.cs:104`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/RhubarbLipSyncTests.cs:46`). A narration or a scene id whose file would leave the media directory is refused before anything runs (`:170`). Each of the JSON's `mouthCues` needs a numeric `start` of at least 0, an `end` after it and a `value` of `A` to `H` or `X` (`src/Scout.Operator/RhubarbLipSync.cs:154`); a cues file newer than the narration is read again without running rhubarb (`:95`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/RhubarbLipSyncTests.cs:184`). A non-zero exit gives rhubarb's last error line as the failure, a timeout or a program that cannot start the runner's sentence, and cues that cannot be read "Scout could not read the mouth cues rhubarb wrote."; each deletes the cues file (`:68`, `:156`). An ffmpeg conversion that exits non-zero gives "Scout could not prepare the narration for lip sync (ffmpeg: <its last line>)" without running rhubarb and leaves neither cues nor wav (`:111`); a cancelled run deletes the cues file and cancels the caller (`src/Scout.Operator/RhubarbLipSync.cs:139`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/RhubarbLipSyncTests.cs:127`). `MouthTrack` (`src/Scout.Operator/MouthTrack.cs:18`) turns the cues into `ffconcat version 1.0` and one `file '<mouths>/<shape>.png'` and `duration <seconds>` per run of equal cues, the first from 0 and each until the next run starts, then the last file once more, because the concat demuxer ignores the last entry's duration; a `'` in the mouths path is written `'\''` (`tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/MouthTrackTests.cs:6`, `:23`). The list is written in the media directory only when the segment is rendered, and deleted after it.

The character is shown still, the picture without a mouth, and the scene's cut carries a note, when the project has no mouth box, "Mark the mouth on the presenter picture in Scout Desktop, Production, so the character speaks.", when the scene has no narration file, "The scene has no narration, so the character is shown still.", when rhubarb or the mouth shapes are missing, the readiness note below, or when rhubarb fails, "Lip sync failed: <the first line of the failure>" (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:388`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:652`, `:764`). Without the presenter picture in the media directory the scene is rendered as a picture scene over its own backdrop, a text card when it has none, with "The project has no presenter picture; ..." (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:142`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:677`); with one ffmpeg reports no size for, or whose measure fails, times out or cannot start, it is rendered the same way with "Scout could not read the presenter picture; attach another one in Scout Desktop, Production.", and the assembly goes on (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:489`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:722`). None of them fails the scene. A scene an 8c-A production saved with visual source `presenter` is never a placeholder for the checks; a scene with its own picture is one, as in `generated_pictures`, only when the picture and the background are both missing (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:565`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/PackageChecksTests.cs:108`). Because the control plane refuses scene changes on a project that is not producing, the notes reach the owner as a seventh check, `presenter`, in a video with presenter scenes only, each cut marking whether the presenter was laid over it (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:183`, `src/Scout.Operator/PackageChecks.cs:47`): `warn` with "Scenes <n>, <m>: <note>" per distinct note, joined by "; ", or `pass` with "The character's mouth moves with the narration in every scene." (`src/Scout.Operator/PackageChecks.cs:45`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/PackageChecksTests.cs:141`, `tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblyTests.cs:100`). The segment name of a presenter scene covers, beyond its command, which carries the `chromakey` of a generated presenter, the backdrop, the presenter picture and the narration by length and write time, the mouth box's four fractions, the mouth track's text by shape names, which carries the cues without the mouths folder, and the mouth set's version, the text of `VERSION` in the mouths folder or else the folder's write time (`src/Scout.Operator/Assembler.cs:160`, `:505`, `src/Scout.Operator/MouthTrack.cs:52`), so a new mouth box, presenter picture, narration, cues or mouth set renders that scene again and nothing else does (`tests/Scout.Operator.Tests/AssemblerTests.cs:693`), a mouths folder the desktop mounts at a new place on a relaunch included (`:781`). A mouth track Scout cannot write in the media directory fails the assembly with "Scout could not save mouth-track-<scene>.txt in the project's media folder." and leaves no temporary file (`:829`).

`ShortsCutter` (`src/Scout.Operator/ShortsCutter.cs:13`) cuts up to three Shorts, `short-1.mp4` and on, from the assembled video (`:28`): each starts where a scene starts and runs over whole following scenes while they fit in 60 s, is kept only at 20 s or more, and a single scene longer than 60 s is cut at 60 s; a lead scene that cannot reach 20 s is skipped and the next scene leads. The frame is 1080x1920: the background is the video cropped to the vertical frame, shrunk to 270x480, blurred with `gblur=sigma=6` and scaled back up (`crop=ih*9/16:ih,scale=270:480,gblur=sigma=6,scale=1080:1920`, `:86`), because `boxblur` is GPL-only and absent from the bundled LGPL ffmpeg; the video fitted across the width in the middle, and the lead scene's first sentence as the hook at the top, wrapped at 22 characters, at most three lines (`:81`). The frame ends with `setsar=1`, because the crop's odd width leaves a non-square sample aspect ratio the overlay would carry into the Short.
