## How it works

`TranscribeAsync` accepts `audio/wav` and `audio/x-wav` (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:272`), `audio/ogg` and `audio/opus`, and throws `NotSupportedException` for anything else before the model loads (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:64`). `WavDecoder.DecodeMono16k` walks the RIFF chunks to the `fmt ` and `data` chunks, skipping any others, and accepts only 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM or 32-bit float, the extensible format included (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WavDecoder.cs:23`, `:70`); the samples go to Whisper as they are, with no Concentus and no resampling. It reads at most 5 minutes of samples (4,800,000, `:97`), straight from the stream into the float array through a pooled 64 KB buffer, with no copy of the data chunk's bytes, and a data chunk cut short gives the samples up to its last whole one (`:108`, `tests/Scout.Transcription.Whisper.Tests/WhisperWavTranscriptionTests.cs:159`); it throws `InvalidDataException` for any other format, a file that is not WAV or one with no samples, which the conductor turns into its "could not transcribe" reply. `OggOpusDecoder.DecodeMono16k` decodes at Opus's native 48 kHz and averages every three samples down to 16 kHz (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/OggOpusDecoder.cs:29`): the managed decoder returns silence when it decodes a SILK (voice-mode) stream straight to 16 kHz, which the committed fixture reproduces. Decoding stops after 5 minutes of audio (`:27`); a stream with no decodable audio throws `InvalidDataException` (`:45`). The model loads on the first call, not when the service is resolved (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:122`); the processor detects the language unless the decode names it and runs on half of the processors (at least one) so a local Ollama is not starved (`:22`), and `TranscribeAsync` joins the segments' text with nothing between them and trims it (`:70`, `:73`); a live decode joins and trims them the same way and hands on the language of the first segment (`:127`, `:130`). A file that is not a ggml model throws `WhisperModelLoadException` when the first decode asks the factory for a builder (`CreateBuilder`; Whisper.net 1.9.1 loads the model inside `WhisperFactory.FromPath` but reports a failed load only from `CreateBuilder`), which the conductor turns into its "could not transcribe" reply. A speech runtime that cannot load fails earlier, inside `WhisperFactory.FromPath`, whose constructor reads the native library first and throws a plain `System.Exception` ("Failed to load native whisper library. Error: ..."), as on a Linux machine without libgomp (read from the 1.9.1 assembly on 2026-11-12). The load goes through `LoadWhisper(modelPath, open)` (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:216`), which rethrows any failure of `open` that is not `WhisperModelLoadException` as the vendor-free `TranscriptionUnavailableException` (`src/Scout.Conversation/Abstractions/IAudioTranscriber.cs:48`) with the sentence "The speech runtime did not load on this computer; on Linux install libgomp (Debian, Ubuntu: libgomp1). The loader's error is in Scout's logs." and the loader's exception as its inner exception (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:223`, `:24`). The action sits inside the first 120 characters, the part of a cause a recording's failure keeps (`src/Scout.Operator/TranscriptionStage.cs:37`); the loader's own text stays in the inner exception and the logs. All three paths load through it, so a voice message, a recording and a live session fail the same way. The conductor answers a voice message that meets it with "Scout cannot transcribe on this computer: the speech runtime did not load. The reason is in Scout's logs." and logs a warning with the exception (`src/Scout.Conversation/ConversationConductor.cs:328`); a recording reports it through the operator's cause sentence and a live session through the local adapter's failed-decode sentence, unchanged. One transcription runs at a time behind a semaphore (`:140`). After each decode, voice or live, a `TimeProvider` timer is re-armed for 10 minutes (`:175`); when it fires and no decode holds the gate, the model is disposed (`:189`) and the next voice message or live window loads it again. Loaded, large-v3-turbo holds about 1.6 GB and large-v3 about 3.1 GB. Model loading and transcription sit behind an internal `LoadedModel` seam, which receives a vendor-free `Decoding` record and yields the segments the model hears, each with its language (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:284`), so the gate, the idle unload and the decode settings are tested without a real model.

`TranscribeSegmentsAsync` reads a 16 kHz mono WAV through the same `WavDecoder`, up to the `maxDuration` the caller gives instead of the voice messages' 5 minutes (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:81`), so a WAV that is not one fails with `InvalidDataException` before the model loads, and yields each segment Whisper hears as `TranscribedSegment(Start, End, Text)`, Whisper.net's own start, end and text (`:266`), no Whisper.net type leaving the project. The gate is taken when the enumeration starts and released, with the idle timer re-armed, only when it ends or is disposed (`:140`, `:172`), so the recording holds the model for its whole transcription ([[pages/services/operator]]). A voice message waits at most 30 seconds for the gate, on the transcriber's `TimeProvider`, and is then refused with `TranscriptionBusyException`, which the conductor answers with "Scout is transcribing a recording right now; try the voice message again in a few minutes."; a recording waits without a bound, and the refused message never takes the gate (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:21`, `:140`, `tests/Scout.Transcription.Whisper.Tests/WhisperAudioTranscriberLifetimeTests.cs:61`, [[pages/decisions/0024-voice-messages-transcribed-locally]]). The processor is released with `DisposeAsync`, which waits for the native run to stop, since its synchronous `Dispose` throws while it processes (`:163`); the operator cancels the run before it releases the segments, however its stage ends. The samples are held in memory for the whole run: 60 minutes are about 230 MB of floats beside the loaded model, read through the 64 KB buffer rather than a 115 MB copy of the WAV's bytes first.

**Live transcription** ([[pages/decisions/0034-live-transcription-on-the-local-adapter]]). `LiveUtteranceSegmenter` turns PCM16 little-endian bytes into float samples (divided by 32,768) and keeps an odd byte and a partial frame across chunks. It checks the RMS of each 20 ms frame (320 samples) against the room (since 2026-11-08, [[pages/decisions/0050-read-aloud-and-conversation-mode]], amendment "a conversation that hears in a noisy room"):

- A frame is speech when its RMS is at least 0.015 and at least three times the room, the quietest frame RMS of the last 150 frames (3 s), counted as at most 0.05 (`Threshold`, `src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/LiveUtteranceSegmenter.cs:182`; `ThresholdOver`, `:184`), so the threshold never rises above 0.15. Without the cap, music at RMS 0.12 set a room that made speech at RMS 0.19 quiet right after it, and also after digital silence, since zero frames do not move the room on. The cost: a room louder than about 0.15 RMS never ends a turn before the 20 s cut. Counting a zero frame as 0.005 instead was rejected: the muted-microphone test fails under it, because 3 s of mute fill the room with 0.005 and the real room at 0.03 then counts as speech. A frame whose RMS is exactly 0, digital silence such as the conversation's muted microphone, is not added to the room (`:115`), so the room heard before Scout answered still holds when the microphone opens again. The room starts as 150 frames at 0.005, so until 3 s of sound have been heard the threshold is the fixed 0.015.

- An utterance opens on the first speech frame and ends after 50 frames that are not speech (1 s), without them; the 1 s is relative to the room, so a room at RMS 0.02 to 0.05 ends a turn 1 s after the speech, and a room that rises during the turn is learned within 3 s.

- An utterance with fewer than 8 speech frames (160 ms) yields nothing. One opened before the room was learned (`:130`) is counted again when it ends, against the room as known then (`HeardEnough`, `:186`), so a noisy room alone, which the fixed threshold took for speech in the first 3 s, never yields a turn to decode. Such a turn's windows wait until it holds 8 frames at least 0.015 and three times the quietest non-zero frame heard so far (`WindowHeard`, `:173`), checked once per frame, so noise in the first 3 s shows no partial while speech, whose syllables dip towards the room, still gets its partial at 0.75 s.

- Windows are cumulative, each the utterance from its start: the first at 12,000 samples (0.75 s), then one every 9,600 more (0.6 s). A window due during a pause waits for speech.

- Each window and each end reaches the session through a callback as it happens, in order (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/LiveUtteranceSegmenter.cs:287`); an end carries why: `Quiet`, `Cap` (20 s) or `Flush` (`LiveTurnEnd`, `:269`). A window's samples are copied only when the session asks, so a dropped window copies nothing.

- An utterance that reaches 320,000 samples (20 s) ends there with those 20 s and a new one starts at the next frame, so no words are dropped; the ring of the same size remains the hard bound.

`WhisperLiveTranscriber` decodes through the voice-message transcriber's model, gate and idle timer (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:93`, `:104`):

- A window is decoded greedily, with no previous-text context and as one segment. Its audio context covers the window and 2 s more, window samples / 320 + 100 from 384 to 1000 (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperAudioTranscriber.cs:308`). It starts only when the gate is free right now and no final waits, and is dropped otherwise, before its samples are copied.

- An ended utterance is decoded again with a beam of 5 and full context. It waits for the gate, in order, and is never dropped.

- A final detects the language. A partial detects it until the session's first final reports one, then is decoded in that language (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperLiveTranscriber.cs:105`). Text is written before the gate is released, so a partial cannot land after its final, and an empty partial is not written.

- Each ended utterance is logged at Information before its decode, with why it ended and its length, never its text: "Live turn ended (Quiet) after 1840 ms of audio; decoding its final text." (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperLiveTranscriber.cs:71`), so the Logs screen shows that speech was heard even when the decode yields nothing. A session that ends without any turn, the flush included and a turn dropped as room noise counted as none, logs at Information "Live session ended with no speech heard (loudest frame RMS 0.003, room 0.003)" when it stops (`src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/WhisperLiveTranscriber.cs:57`), so a microphone too quiet for a turn, or one sending only digital silence (loudest 0.000), shows there too. The numbers carry a dot in every culture. The room printed is the measured one, not capped at 0.05; before 3 s of sound it is at most 0.005, the starting value, which is not a measurement.

- `StopAsync` flushes the open utterance as one final and waits for every decode. A decode failure, such as a model that fails to load, is rethrown, the same exception instance, on the next `FeedAsync` or by `StopAsync`.
