## Production

- **Project detail** (`:552`), on a click, from `scout:operator.project`. Its title row (`project-title-row`, `:579`) holds the topic over its status chip and "<format> · updated <time>" and, at its right, Delegate, labelled "Let Scout finish it" with the title "Scout finishes the video on its own and asks you only at approval", Delete (`project-title-delete`, the prototype's, opening the same dialog as the row's trash and hidden on the same statuses) and a close mark (`project-close`, `:594`, labelled "Close the project"), which closes the detail. Under the title "Voice: <the readiness label of the project's voice, else its id>", or "Voice: Scout's choice" while it is empty (`data-test` `project-voice`, `:586`, `:2884`), a "Delegated" badge on a delegated project (`:590`), then "Format: Generated pictures", "Format: Your own pictures", "Format: Your own video" or "Format: Animated character" (`data-test` `project-format`, `:593`, `:85`), "Visual: <Still|Gentle|Dynamic> motion" (`data-test` `project-visual`, `:594`, `:2898`) and, when the project has a direction, the direction as text under it (`project-direction`, `:596`), the project's error in the failure style only on a `failed` project (`project-failure`, `:600`) and otherwise as a plain note (`project-note`, `:602`), so the note of an animated character project whose presenter picture could not be made, kept on a `media_ready` project, does not read as a failure, Then the blocks follow the work: Topic candidates, Script, Claims, Presenter (Animated character only), Scenes with the Produce panel, then Background, or Recording in a Your own video project, each title with its help mark. Topic candidates (`candidates`, `:606`) is a `<details>` open while the project has no script and closed once it has one, with each candidate's title, fit as a percentage and reason, or "No topic candidates yet."; the script (hook, sections with heading and narration, in a Your own video project each under its "<m:ss> to <m:ss>" in the recording, `script-section-time`, `:654`, call to action, title variants, description, tags, chapters), or "The script is not written yet.". The presenter description is no longer in the header: it is in the Presenter block.

- **Claims** (`claims`, `:681`) - a `<details>` open while any claim is `pending`, when a click on its title is cancelled so it cannot be folded (`:682`), and closed once every claim is decided; with no claims it reads "No claims to check.". Its title is "Claims to check" and, while any is pending, carries "<n> waiting for you" (`claims-pending`, `:695`). Pending claims come first (`claims-open`, `:703`, `pendingOf`, `:2773`); the decided ones fold under "Decided (<n>)" (`claims-decided`, `:709`, `decidedOf`, `:2777`), a `<details>` with no bound state, closed at first. Each claim (`:724`) has a stripe on its left edge by risk, red for `high`, amber for `medium` and green for `low` (`claim--<risk>`), and a chip "High risk", "Medium risk" or "Low risk" in the same colour (`claim-risk-<id>`, `:739`), then "Status: <status>" with the owner's note, its text, and each source as a chip with the URL's host (`claim-source-<id>-<i>`, `:755`, `domainOf`, `:2782`, the text as it came when it is not a URL), the whole URL as its title, and "Copy link" (`claim-copy-<id>-<i>`, `:761`), which copies the whole URL (`:3303`). The chip is not a link and nothing opens it: the URLs come from the model and the web, and `scout:app.openExternal` allows only a fixed list of hosts. A pending claim, or a decided one after its "Change" (`claim-change-<id>`, `:803`), which reopens that claim's decision until the next decision or another project (`reopenedClaim`, `:2541`), has one segmented control (`role` `group`, `:769`) named "Decision on the claim: <text>", of Supported, Not supported and Needs no source, each with its glyph (`supported-<id>`, `unsupported-<id>`, `waive-<id>`), the current status pressed (`aria-pressed`). Supported and Unsupported send that status to `scout:operator.decideClaim`; Waive opens a note field (`waive-note-<id>`, `:816`) and "Waive claim" stays disabled until the note has text, then sends `waived` with the trimmed note (`:2742`). A refusal shows under the claims (`claim-error`).

- **Delegate** (`:559`), in the title row - on a `script_ready` or `media_ready` project, or a `failed` one with a script (`:46`, `:2843`), the control plane's rule, whatever claims are still pending, "Delegate" opens a dialog (`data-test` `delegate-dialog`, `:1774`, `:2956`) with the same narration voice select as the Produce panel (`delegate-voice`, preselected the same way), its own Preview (`delegate-voice-preview`, `:1805`), the same Direction and Motion (`delegate-brief` with `delegate-brief-count`, `delegate-motion`, `:1817`, `:1843`, prefilled the same way), the same warning as the Produce panel for the project's format (`:1855`), what will run, "Produce media", "Assemble the video" and "Stop at ready to approve: publishing needs your approval" (`:1858`), and Cancel and "Confirm and delegate", which sends the project id, the chosen voice, the trimmed direction and the motion to `scout:operator.delegation` (`:2965`). In an `animated_character` project it also has "Presenter" (`delegate-presenter`, `:1830`) prefilled with the project's presenter description, with its "<n> / 300" count (`delegate-presenter-count`), and sends it trimmed as `presenter`; every other format sends an empty one, which keeps the project's. In an `own_video` project the dialog, like the Produce panel, has no voice select, Preview or Motion and sends an empty voice and `gentle` (`:1778`, `:1839`). The answer replaces the project, queued for production or for assembly and delegated, and closes the dialog; a refusal keeps it open with the control plane's reason, "The owner has not allowed youtube to write; allow it on the Features screen of Scout Desktop." without the grant. The control plane runs the production and the assembly and stops at the package approval, so publishing stays the owner's ([[pages/services/control-plane]], Delegation).

- **Delete** - a trash icon (`data-test` `project-delete-<id>`, labelled "Delete <topic>", `:550`) sits on the project's row in the Projects list, at the right of the date, a sibling of the row button so a click opens the dialog without selecting the project (`openDelete`, `:3049`), and is hidden while the status is `researching`, `scripting`, `transcribing`, `producing`, `assembling` or `presenting` (`RUNNING`, `:50`), or the publication status is `uploading`. It opens a dialog (`delete-dialog`, `:1886`, rendered for the project being deleted whether or not it is the open one) titled 'Delete "<topic>"?' and saying "This removes the project, its script, scenes and media files from Scout's data folder. A video already on YouTube stays there. It cannot be undone.", which describes the dialog, as an alert dialog with Cancel focused when it opens, so Enter never deletes (`openDelete` focuses it after the render), Escape closes it without deleting (`:1887`) as the line under the buttons says, "Escape or Cancel closes this without deleting." (`delete-hint`, `:1919`), and "Delete project" (`delete-confirm`) painted as danger with its trash icon, which sends the project id to `scout:operator.projectDeletion`. On the answer `{ deleted: true }` the dialog closes, the project leaves the list and the detail closes when it was the open one; a refusal keeps the dialog with the control plane's reason (`delete-error`), "A project that is researching, scripting, transcribing, producing, assembling or uploading cannot be deleted; wait for it to finish or fail." or "The project has pending YouTube actions; finish or cancel them first.". Deleting needs no grant ([[pages/services/control-plane]]).
