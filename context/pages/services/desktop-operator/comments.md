## Comments

The Comments page (route `operator/comments`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/app.routes.ts:111`, after Experiments and before Approvals in the navigation, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/operator/operator-shell.component.ts:23`; `CommentsComponent`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/comments/comments.component.ts:585`) is the desktop side of phase 6 of [[pages/roadmap/youtube-channel-operator]]: the owner reads the comments the worker synced, approves replies and holds, and turns audience requests into videos. The worker syncs, classifies and drafts ([[pages/services/operator]]); the control plane holds the comments and decides what is allowed ([[pages/services/control-plane]]). It reads `scout:operator.comments` on open and every 10 s while open (`:21`, `:731`); a failed load shows the error and keeps the last view. Before the first read it shows a loading block named "Loading comments"; with no comment and no idea, "No comments on your channel yet. Scout reads new ones each time it syncs." with Sync now. The lists below are five tabs with their counts, Waiting for you, Replies, Other comments, Quarantine and Audience ideas, over the theme filter, each keeping its empty line; the reply and hold dialogs are `MatDialog` on the theme's card. Each answer of an action replaces the view; a refusal shows the control plane's reason as it came.

- **Sync** (`:89`) - at the header's right, "Sync now", which sends `scout:operator.commentSync`, over "Last synced today at <HH:mm>, London time" (or the date) or "Not synced yet.", and the last sync's error in the failure style; while the control plane has a sync request stamped the button is disabled and reads "Sync requested" over "Scout syncs within a minute." (`:103`), and it is disabled until the first read.
- **Themes** (`:117`) - a chip "<theme> (<n>)" per theme, counted over the comments that are not quarantined, held or dismissed, most first and then by name, empty themes left out (`:611`); a chip shows only its theme's comments in the lists below, and "All" shows every one again; a filter whose theme no longer counts a comment clears itself (`:734`).
- **Waiting for you** (`:147`, the tab that was the Questions and requests section) - every comment that is not spam and is new, draft requested, drafted or failed, and is a question or a request, or has a draft or a draft request, or a reply that failed (`:49`): the author, the video title, the published time in London as "Today, <HH:mm>", "Yesterday, <HH:mm>" or the date, the kind in words, "Draft waiting for your approval" on a drafted one, and the text, "Draft requested; the Scout worker writes it within a minute." while it waits, the reply's error after a failed reply, and a textarea labelled "Your reply (drafted by Scout, edit freely)", or "Your reply" without a draft, with the worker's draft and "<n> / 1000" of the trimmed text (`:176`). The textarea keeps the owner's unsaved text across the polling until the worker's draft changes, which replaces it (`:649`, `:732`), as Assembly's metadata form does. "Approve reply" is disabled on an empty or over-long text; it opens "Approve the reply?", saying "Scout posts this reply to <author> on "<video title>" within a minute. You can cancel it until then.", quoting the exact trimmed text and naming the action, "Action: post a public reply on YouTube" (`:410`), and "Approve and post" sends the comment id and that trimmed text to `scout:operator.replyApproval` (`:704`). A refusal keeps the dialog open with its reason. Dismiss sends `dismiss` to `scout:operator.commentDecision`.
- **Replies** (`:207`) - each approved, publishing, posted or failed reply with its approved text and its state as a chip: "Waiting to post", "Posting", "Posted on YouTube" with the link `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<video id>&lc=<reply id>` with both ids encoded (`:663`), opened through `scout:app.openExternal`, whose allow-list checks only the protocol and the host `www.youtube.com` (`ui/packages/main/src/ipc/app.ts:36`), or "Failed" with the reply's error. A reply waiting to publish and a hold waiting to run each have Cancel, "Cancel before it posts" on the reply (`:220`, `:334`), which sends `dismiss` to `scout:operator.commentDecision`, so the control plane cancels the approved action and marks the comment dismissed; a publishing reply has none, because the control plane refuses it there, and a refusal shows its reason.
- **Other comments** (`:253`) - the rest that are new and not spam, with Draft a reply (`draft`) and Dismiss; a comment the owner asks a draft for moves to Questions and requests.
- **Quarantine** (`:292`) - the quarantined comments, with Not spam (`not_spam`) and Hold for review on YouTube, whose dialog, "Hold the comment on YouTube?", says what YouTube does and names the action, "Action: hold a comment for review on YouTube" (`:437`), before "Hold the comment" sends `hold`; a hold's state is a chip, "Waiting to hold", "Held for review on YouTube", or "Failed" with its error and Dismiss (`:59`).
- **Audience ideas** (`:366`) - each proposed idea's text, with "Asked by <author> on "<video title>"" when its comment is in the view, with "Make a video" (`use` true) and Dismiss (`use` false) on `scout:operator.ideaDecision` (`:622`).

Every comment's text, author and theme reaches the page through Angular interpolation only, never `innerHTML`; there is no avatar, no author link and no change to the content security policy. Main does not write the reply or the hold: it hands the owner's decision to the control plane, which records it and queues the one approved action the worker runs ([[pages/decisions/0032-per-feature-write-permissions]]).

In main, `scout:operator.comments` and `scout:operator.commentSync` take nothing; `scout:operator.commentDecision` refuses a decision other than `draft`, `dismiss`, `not_spam` and `hold` ("A comment is decided as draft, dismiss, not_spam or hold.") and a comment id that does not match `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,100}$` after trimming ("'<id>' is not a comment id.", `ui/packages/main/src/ipc/operator.ts:151`, `:189`); `scout:operator.replyApproval` takes the same id and a text of 1 to 1000 characters after trimming ("A reply is 1 to 1000 characters.") and forwards the trimmed text; `scout:operator.ideaDecision` needs an idea id and a boolean `use` ("An idea is used or dismissed."), all before the control plane (`:917` to `:939`). Every other rule, a control character in a reply among them, is the control plane's, and its refusal reaches the renderer unchanged. The client calls `ListComments`, `RequestCommentSync`, `DecideComment`, `ApproveReply` and `DecideIdea` with the operator error mapping above (`ui/packages/main/src/control-plane/client.ts:1076`); `toComments` (`:778`) maps every absent optional field, the approved text, the reply id, the error, the decision time, an idea's project id and the sync's three times, to `null`. Every channel of the client, to Providers, Features and Operator, sends and receives gRPC messages of up to 16 MB (`:470`), the size the worker and the control plane allow, because a full comments view at the caps, 1000 comments of up to 2000 characters with their drafts and replies, passes gRPC's 4 MB default. The DTOs are `AudienceCommentDto`, `AudienceIdeaDto`, `CommentsViewDto`, `CommentDecisionRequest`, `ReplyApprovalRequest` and `IdeaDecisionRequest` (`ui/packages/shared/src/ipc-contract.ts:961`, `:982`, `:991`, `:1001`, `:1007`, `:1012`).
