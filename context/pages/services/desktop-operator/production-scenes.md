## Production

- **Scenes** (`:1136`; since 2026-11-10 each scene is the prototype's card: the 200-pixel 16:9 picture, then "Scene <n>" with its status chip, Ready, Making, Failed or Not made yet, and "<picture source> · narration: <audio source>", the narration, and the row New picture, Edit text, the round Move up and Move down named "Move scene <n> up" and "down", and More; the audio player stays under the narration), in the project detail after the Presenter block, or after Claims. On a `script_ready`, `media_ready`, `assembled` or `approved` project (`:36`), or a `failed` one with a script, as the control plane allows (`:2762`), "Produce media" (`:1153`) opens the Produce panel under it (`data-test` `produce-panel`, `:1167`, `:2926`); while any claim is `pending` it stays disabled and says "<n> claim(s) is/are still waiting; decide every claim before producing the media." in the warn colour, which describes the button (`:1160`). The panel's narration voice select (`produce-voice`, `:1172`) is built from the voices the worker reported in the readiness: "Scout's choice" first (empty, so the provider speech when it has it, else the bundled Cori voice), then one group per source in the order the readiness lists them, each option showing the voice label (`:2561`). The project's voice is preselected (`:2927`); a voice the readiness does not list, or any voice while the readiness has not loaded, is shown as a disabled but selected option "<id> (not available on this machine)" (`:2889`), so Produce sends the stored id back unchanged, the worker reports the unavailable voice on the scene, and the owner can still pick a listed voice. Beside it, "Preview" (`produce-voice-preview`, `:1195`) plays the selected voice's sample: it sets the page's one `<audio>` element (`voice-sample-player`, `:1892`) to `scout-media://voice-samples/<sampleFile>`, the name encoded, and plays it (`:2914`). For Scout's choice it plays nothing while the readiness lists a provider voice, because the video then uses the provider's speech, and otherwise the default bundled voice's sample, the first `piper` voice whose id contains `cori`, else the first `piper` voice, as `PiperSpeech.Default` picks (`:2904`). It is disabled with the title "No sample yet" when that voice has no `sampleFile`, a provider voice or a sample not made yet, and for Scout's choice while a provider voice is listed; the readiness line "Voices: <n> available" is unchanged. Under the voice, "Direction" (`produce-brief`, `:1207`) is a textarea with "<n> / 1000" of the trimmed text (`produce-brief-count`), prefilled with the project's `direction` when it has one, else the saved Mandate's voice and style (`:2922`), so the owner can still change the direction until production, and "Motion" (`produce-motion`, `:1233`) offers Still, Gentle (recommended) and Dynamic (`still`, `gentle`, `dynamic`, `:73`), preselected from the project's motion. The panel does not stop a direction over 1000 characters; main refuses it with the sentence below. Once the readiness has loaded, the panel shows a warning for the selected project's format (`data-test` `produce-warning`, `:1245`, built at `:2568`), naming the provider ("The connected AI Source" when it is empty). A Generated pictures project shows, with no speech and no picture source, "<Provider> has no speech or image service in Scout: every scene will be a silent text card. Replace the narration on each scene and attach a background afterwards, or connect an AI Source with speech and pictures first."; with no speech or image service but stock pictures, "... every scene will be silent and pictures come from Openverse, credited. Replace the narration on each scene afterwards, or connect an AI Source with speech first."; with pictures but no speech, "<Provider> has no speech service in Scout: every scene will be silent. Replace the narration on each scene afterwards, or connect an AI Source with speech first."; with speech and no picture source, "<Provider> has no image service in Scout: every scene will be a text card. Attach a background afterwards, or connect an AI Source with pictures first."; with speech and pictures or stock pictures, none. A Your own pictures project shows only the no-speech sentence, and nothing while speech is available, because its pictures are the owner's (`:2584`); an Animated character project (`:2574`) shows, with no speech, "<Provider> has no speech service in Scout: the character will be silent. Replace the narration on each scene afterwards, or connect an AI Source with speech first.", with speech but no image service and no presenter picture, "<Provider> has no image service in Scout: attach a presenter picture, or every scene will be a text card.", and otherwise none, and its panel also has "Presenter" (`produce-presenter`, `:1220`) with its "<n> / 300" count, prefilled with the project's presenter description (`:2930`) and sent trimmed as `presenter`, every other format sending an empty one; a Your own video project shows none (`:2572`), and its panel has no narration voice, Preview or Motion (`:1168`, `:1229`), so Produce sends the project's stored voice and motion back unchanged. "Produce" sends the project id, the chosen voice, the trimmed direction and the motion to `scout:operator.production` (`:2935`) and closes the panel; a refusal shows the control plane's reason under it and keeps it open; Cancel closes it and sends nothing. The panel and the Delegate dialog belong to their project id and keep the chosen voice, direction and motion in their own state, so the 10 s refresh neither closes them nor resets what the owner chose; choosing a project closes both (`:2705`). A `presenting` project, the worker making its presenter, shows "Making the presenter." (`presenting-progress`, `:1273`), a `producing` one "<n> of <m> scenes ready" (`:1276`), and a `transcribing` one "Transcribed <m:ss> of <m:ss>", the end of the last transcript section the worker saved over the recording's length (`transcription-progress`, `:1281`, `:2871`). The scenes are listed by their `order` (`:1288`), each a card (`scene-<id>`, `:1290`) with the picture on the left (`:1291`) and the rest beside it (`:1382`), under the picture when the card is narrow: its narration, in a Your own pictures project "Picture prompt: ...", in a Generated pictures or Animated character project the picture prompt on one line, cut with an ellipsis and whole in its title (`scene-picture-prompt-line-<id>`, `:1394`), with "Edit prompt" (`scene-picture-prompt-edit-<id>`, `:1401`, off on a locked scene), which opens an editable textarea in its place (`scene-picture-prompt-<id>`, `:1413`) with Save (`scene-picture-prompt-save-<id>`), disabled while the trimmed text is empty, over 700 characters, unchanged, or the scene locked, the sentence "A picture prompt is 1 to 700 characters." in the failure style while it is empty or over 700 (`scene-picture-prompt-invalid-<id>`), and Cancel (`scene-picture-prompt-cancel-<id>`), which drops the edit, closes the textarea and sends nothing; Save sends the project id, the scene id and the trimmed prompt to `scout:operator.scenePicturePrompt` (`:3243`) and closes it on the answer, the edit belonging to its scene id so the 10 s refresh keeps it and choosing a project drops it; in a Your own video project "Clip: <m:ss> to <m:ss>" in its place, `h:mm:ss` past the first hour the way the chapters read (`scene-clip-<id>`, `:1385`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:2889`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.spec.ts:2073`), the status badge (`planned`, `generating`, `ready`, `failed`), "Audio: generated | your file | your recording | none · Picture: generated | your file | Openverse | text card | your recording" (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:68`), under a picture with a credit a line "Credit: <text>" (`data-test` `scene-credit-<id>`, from the scene's `visualCredit`, null when absent), its error in the failure style only on a `failed` scene (`:1473`) and otherwise as a plain note (`data-test` `scene-note-<id>`, `:1475`), so a fallback note on a `ready` scene does not read as a failure, an image preview with the alt text "Picture of scene <order>" with the 1-based order when the scene has its own picture (`:1326`, `:1328`), and otherwise a 16:9 preview of the card the assembler draws (`data-test` `scene-preview-<id>`, `:1334`): the first sentence of the narration, after control characters and runs of whitespace collapse to one space, up to the first `.`, `!` or `?` that is followed by whitespace or the end (so `.NET` and `1.5` inside the sentence do not end it, as in the assembler) and else all of it, trimmed (`:2900`), in white, centred on the dark `#14181f` card, or at the bottom over the project's background. A recording scene has no picture file, so it shows that card too, though the assembler shows the recording with the scene's text as captions. In the scene's action row, "New picture" (the old Regenerate picture, `scene-picture-regenerate-<id>`, `:1370`, off on a locked scene) on a Generated pictures or Animated character project, which sends the scene to `scout:operator.scenePictureRegeneration` (`:3248`) so the control plane clears the picture and keeps the narration, and "Attach image" (`scene-attach-visual-<id>`, `:1381`) on a Your own pictures or Animated character project (`:1363`). Beside the picture, the narration row (`data-test` `scene-audio-row-<id>`, `:1479`, in both picture formats and never in a Your own video project, `:1478`) holds the audio player with controls when the scene has a file (`scene-audio-<id>`, `:1483`), "Replace narration" (`scene-narration-replace-<id>`, `:1490`), which attaches the owner's audio file as the old Attach audio did (there is no Attach audio button any more), and the "More actions for scene <n>" menu (`scene-narration-menu-<id>`, `:1496`), a round `<details>` closed at first, the prototype's More, whose items are Attach image, Edit prompt, Replace narration, Lock or Unlock, then "Regenerate scene" (`scene-regenerate-<id>`, `:1506`, off on a locked scene) on a Generated pictures or Animated character project and "Regenerate narration" (`scene-narration-regenerate-<id>`, `:1516`, off on a locked scene), which sends the scene to `scout:operator.narrationRegeneration` (`:3295`) and, on a scene whose audio is the owner's own, first asks "Replace your own narration on this scene with Scout's voice? Your file stays on disk but the video will use the generated narration." in a dialog (`narration-replace-dialog`, `:1708`, `askRegenerateNarration` at `:3284`) where Cancel sends nothing. Then the row of "Edit text" (`scene-edit-<id>`, `:1530`, off on a locked scene) in every format, Lock or Unlock (`:1539`), Move up and Move down (off at the ends). Your own pictures and Your own video scenes keep their controls; the background block is the same in both picture formats. An Animated character scene has every control of a Generated pictures scene and Attach image, as phase 8c-B gives each scene its own backdrop: with a picture of its own, its 16:9 preview is that picture filling the card (`scene-image-<id>`) with the project's presenter picture over it in the lower right at 45 percent of the height, as the assembler places it (`scene-presenter-badge-<id>`, `:1317`); without one, the project's presenter picture, scaled to fit (`object-fit: contain`), over the background or the dark card (`scene-preview-presenter-<id>`, `:1347`), or the text card with the first sentence while the project has no presenter picture. Edit text opens, under the scene, in a Your own video project the line "The new text replaces this scene's captions; the recording is not changed." (`scene-text-hint-<id>`, `:1567`), and a textarea prefilled with the narration (`scene-text-<id>`, `:1574`), "<n> / 700" of the trimmed text (`scene-text-count-<id>`, `:1578`), the sentence "A scene's text is 1 to 700 characters." in the failure style while the trimmed text is empty or over 700 (`scene-text-invalid-<id>`, `:1582`), Save (`scene-text-save-<id>`, `:1590`), disabled then and while the trimmed text equals the narration, and Cancel (`scene-text-cancel-<id>`, `:1603`), which closes it and sends nothing. Save sends the project id, the scene id and the trimmed text to `scout:operator.sceneText` (`:3307`) and closes the editor on the answer; a refusal keeps it open and shows the control plane's reason under the scenes (`scene-error`, `:1618`). The editor belongs to one scene id, so the 10 s refresh keeps it and choosing a project closes it. The control plane resets that scene's narration and queues the project for production on both a text edit and a narration regeneration, keeping its picture, so the next run narrates it again ([[pages/services/control-plane]]). Move sends every scene id in the new order to `scout:operator.sceneOrder` (`:3233`); Lock sends the flipped flag to `scout:operator.sceneLock` (`:3223`); Regenerate scene sends the scene to `scout:operator.sceneRegeneration` (`:3242`); Replace narration and Attach image send the project id, the scene id and `audio` or `visual` to `scout:operator.sceneMedia` and nothing else (`:3320`). Each answer replaces the project in the detail and the list; a refusal shows its reason under the scenes, and a closed file dialog changes nothing.
