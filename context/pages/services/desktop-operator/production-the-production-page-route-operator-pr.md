## Production

The Production page (route `operator/production` in the operator window, see Operator window above, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/app.routes.ts:86`) is the desktop side of the channel operator ([[pages/roadmap/youtube-channel-operator]] phases 2, 3, 7, 8a and 8b, [[pages/decisions/0033-channel-operator-architecture]]), laid out since 2026-11-05 around the project the owner works on ([[pages/roadmap/production-screen]]). It reads and edits the operator's state in the control plane and holds no pipeline logic; the worker researches, writes the script and makes the media. `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:2509` has these parts:

- **Channel** (`:163`) - one `<details>` (`data-test` `channel`) holding the Mandate and What Scout can make, open while no mandate is saved and closed once one is: the binding is `mandate() === null`, so it is also open before the mandate loads and after a failed load, whose error shows inside it. The mandate form and its Save show only once the mandate has been read (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:206`), so a read that failed never offers an empty form to save over a saved mandate. A mandate or readiness read the control plane did not answer does not show in the Mandate, Channel or readiness banners: it is held in its own signal (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:2563`, `:2564`) and shows once, in the page banner above the sections (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:163`), next to the list's own failure; any other failure of those reads shows in its block (`loadFailed`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:3408`). Angular writes `open` only when that value changes, so a block the owner opens stays open until a mandate is saved for the first time. Its summary is the plain title Channel (no heading inside the summary, `:169`) and the line (`channel-summary`, `:170`, with the readiness failure's message beside it as `channel-error` while one is set, so a folded Channel still shows it) reads "Mandate saved <d MMM, HH:mm> · Default format: <format name>", Generated pictures while the mandate names none, or "No mandate yet", then, once the readiness has loaded, "· Can make: " and the short labels of `makes` (`:2788`): in lower case as the prototype writes them: narration with speech, generated pictures with images, stock pictures with stock pictures, animated character with lip sync and your own video with speech-to-text, joined by commas, or "text cards only". Opened, the mandate's fields sit in the prototype's grid (columns of at least 220 pixels, each label at 12 px above its field, the five pillars on one row).

- **Mandate** (`:182`), first inside Channel, with its help mark (`help-mandate`, `:188`) - objective, audience, up to five pillars, cadence, language, voice and style (the `mandate-style` input, `:251`), blocked topics as a comma list, the target metric (Views, Watch hours, Subscribers or Engagement, stored as `views`, `watch_hours`, `subscribers`, `engagement`, `:139`) the target value and the default format (`mandate-default-format`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:293`), a select of Generated pictures, Your own pictures, Animated character and Your own video, stored as the format's value and showing Generated pictures while the mandate has none. The narration voice is not part of the mandate: it belongs to each video and is chosen when the owner produces it (see Scenes). Save sends every field trimmed, the filled pillars only and the blocked topics split on commas to `scout:operator.saveMandate` (`:2680`), then shows "Saved <yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm>". With no mandate saved: "The operator needs a mandate before it makes a video: what the channel is for, who it is for and its language. Fill it in and save it." A refused save shows the reason.

- **What Scout can make** (`:320`), inside Channel after the Mandate, has no help mark (the design gives it no text) - the readiness the worker last reported, read once on open from `scout:operator.readiness` (`:3328`): "Narration: available" or "not available", "Generated pictures: available" or "not available", a pictures line (`data-test` `readiness-stock`): "Pictures: generated by <provider>" when images are available, else "Pictures: from Openverse (CC0, public domain or CC BY, credited in the description)" when the worker reported stock pictures, else "Pictures: text cards unless you attach your own", the provider ("none" when empty), "YouTube: signed in" or "not signed in", "Voices: <n> available" or "Voices: none" (`data-test` `readiness-voices`, `:335`), and each note. Before the worker reports, the control plane answers all false with the note "The Scout worker has not reported yet."

- **Make a video** (`:357`, its heading labels the section and has no help mark, as the prototype draws it) - one row, the topic with its visible label Topic, the example "For example: why your SSD slows down when it is full" and the line under the row describing it, "Scout researches the topic, writes the script and lists the claims to check. You can also ask for a video in chat." (`topic`, `:369`), and Start (`start-project`, `:377`), then "More options: format and direction" (`start-options`, `:395`), a `<details>` closed at first that holds the format, the direction and the presenter description, so a start with it closed uses the preselected format, the mandate's default. The format is a radio group drawn as the prototype's cards (stroke icon, name and one-line blurb; a card that is off is dashed on surface-2 with its reason under the blurb and describes its radio by blurb and reason; the chosen card has the accent border and ring and its radio checked), in the order Generated pictures, Your own pictures, Animated character, Podcast, AI presenter, Your own video; Podcast is always off with "Coming later." since the format is not in this version, and the arrow keys move the choice through the formats that are on, round the ends. It starts on the saved mandate's default format, or on Generated pictures while the mandate has none or names a format the machine cannot make now (Animated character without lip sync, Your own video without speech-to-text), whose radio stays greyed with its reason (`preferredFormat`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:2572`); the owner's own choice wins until the start (`name` `start-format`): "Generated pictures" (`format-generated_pictures`) and "Your own pictures" (`format-own_pictures`, `:416`), then "Animated character" (`format-animated_character`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/production/production.component.ts:426`), enabled only while the readiness says the worker has lip sync (`lipSyncAvailable`, `:2570`), before it loads included, and otherwise disabled with "Needs the lip-sync tool, which is not installed on this computer." under it (`format-animated_character-reason`, `:438`); each reason under a disabled format describes its radio, then "AI presenter" (`format-ai_presenter`, `:446`), always disabled, with the line "The connected AI Source has no video model." under it (`format-ai_presenter-reason`, `:455`), then "Your own video" (`format-own_video`, `:462`), enabled only while the readiness says the worker has a speech-to-text model (`transcriptionAvailable`, `:2569`), before it loads included, and otherwise disabled with "Scout has no speech-to-text model on this machine; download one in Models, Transcription." under it (`format-own_video-reason`, `:474`). Direction is a labelled textarea with "How the video should feel: tone, audience, what to stress or avoid." and the example "Plain words, one analogy per idea, end with a checklist", described by that line and by "<n> / 1,000" of the trimmed text (`start-direction-count`, `:494`). While Animated character is chosen, a field "Presenter" (`start-presenter-field`, `:499`), a labelled textarea for who the character is and where they are, with "Who appears on screen: look, age, clothing, setting. Scout keeps the same character in every scene.", the example "A friendly woman in her thirties, glasses, a navy blazer, a bright studio" and "<n> / 300" of the trimmed text (`start-presenter-count`); it is hidden for every other format. The limits stay 1000 and 300; only Make a video shows them with a thousands separator. The button is disabled under 3 characters of topic, while the trimmed direction is over 1000 and, in Animated character, while the trimmed presenter description is over 300 (`:380`); it sends the trimmed topic, the chosen format, the trimmed direction and, in Animated character, the trimmed presenter description (empty in any other format) to `scout:operator.startProject`, then shows the queued project's topic and status under the row (`queued`, `:521`) and resets the topic, the format to that default, the direction and the presenter description (`:2706`). The format is the project's for good: no screen changes it after the start. Without the owner's YouTube write grant the control plane refuses the start, and the reason, "The owner has not allowed youtube to write; allow it on the Features screen of Scout Desktop.", shows under the field.

- **Projects** (`projects`, `:523`) - the last 50 from `scout:operator.projects`, newest created first (`:3362`), each with its topic, its status as a chip and its updated time. Each row reads the topic, the status in words on its tone's chip ("Script ready for review", "Making the media", "On YouTube"; a status the prototype does not name reads with its underscores as spaces) and the time, "Today, HH:mm" or "d MMM, HH:mm", followed by "· <n> of <m> scenes ready" while producing and "· <error>" on a failed project. Before the first read, "Loading projects" with a bar and four skeleton rows; a failed first read leaves the heading alone. Empty: the round mark, "No videos yet. Start one above, or ask Scout in chat." and Start a video, which, like New video in the page header, puts the focus on the topic without leaving the page" A click on a project opens its detail; a click on the open one, whose button is `aria-pressed` (`:537`), closes it (`select`, `:2705`, `close`, `:2732`). The list and the detail share one grid (`work`, `:522`), with the class `work--open` while a project is open: while the page itself is at least 960 pixels wide, a container query on the page (`:2139`), the list sits beside the detail in a 220 to 300 pixel column, and below that it sits above it. The page, not the window, is measured because the operator window's minimum is 960 pixels and its navigation takes part of it.
