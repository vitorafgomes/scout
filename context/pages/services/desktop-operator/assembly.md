## Assembly

The Assembly page (route `operator/assembly`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/app.routes.ts:97`, between Production and Approvals in the operator window's navigation, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/operator/operator-shell.component.ts:20`) is the desktop side of phase 4 of [[pages/roadmap/youtube-channel-operator]]: the owner asks for the assembled video, watches it, checks it, edits its upload metadata, confirms the media rights and approves the package. The worker renders; the control plane decides what is allowed. `AssemblyComponent` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:714`):

- **Picker** (`:68`) - a select of the last 50 projects whose status is `media_ready`, `assembly_queued`, `assembling`, `assembled`, `approved` or `failed` (`:26`), newest created first, each as "<topic> · <status in words>" (Waiting to assemble, Queued for assembly, Assembling, Assembled, Approved, Failed), after "Choose a video", with the chosen project's status as the shared status chip beside the select. Before the first read the page shows a loading card named "Loading videos". With none: "No video is ready for assembly yet. Make its media on the Production page first." and Go to Production, a link to the Production page, which Open in Production beside the title also opens.
- **Assemble video** (`:85`) - "Assemble video", filled, or, once the package has a video, "Assemble again", outlined and described by "Assembled <when>. Assembling again replaces the video, Shorts and thumbnail below.", or without its first sentence for a package with no `assembledAt` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:224`); enabled on a `media_ready` or `assembled` project, or a `failed` one with at least one scene and every scene `ready` (`:796`), the control plane's own rule; it sends the project id to `scout:operator.assembly` (`:899`) and a refusal shows the control plane's reason. While the project is `assembly_queued` the page says "Queued for assembly; the Scout worker starts it next.", while `assembling` "Assembling the video, its Shorts and its thumbnail." (`:93`, `:97`). Under it the scenes in order, "<n> scenes · <m> min <s> s", each a tile "Scene <n>" over its start time.
- **Package**, once the owner first asked for assembly: the package error in the failure style (`:108`), the video in a `<video controls>` (`:116`), each Short with its own player and its hook (`:131`), and the thumbnail with the package's `thumbnailDescription` as its alt text, "Thumbnail of the video" without one (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:274`), each read through `scout-media` with `?v=<updatedAt>` so a new render with the same name is fetched again (`:885`). "No Shorts yet." while there are none. The thumbnail and the Shorts sit beside the video.
- **Checks** (`:154`) - a table of each check the worker ran: its name, its result as a Passed, Warning or Failed chip, its detail. "No checks yet." while there are none.
- **YouTube details**, on an `assembled` or `approved` project - the title with a "<n> / 100" counter of the trimmed text (`:191`), the description with "<n> / 5000", the tags as chips with a remove button each and a field whose Enter or "Add tag" adds the tags typed there, separated by commas, skipping one already listed, with "<n> / 500" over all tags counted YouTube's way, the control plane's count, two more for a tag with a space and a comma between tags (`:213`, `:772`). "Save details" (`:259`), with "Saved <when>" beside it once the owner's edits are saved, "Saved" for a package with no `metadataSavedAt` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:461`), stays disabled while the title is empty or a limit is broken (`:767`) and sends the trimmed title and description and the tags to `scout:operator.packageMetadata` (`:903`). The form takes the package's title, description and tags when another project is chosen and whenever the package's metadata changes, never on a refresh that leaves it as it was (`:945`), so unsaved edits survive the polling. Saving on an approved package returns it to `assembled` without its approval; the page shows the project the control plane returns.
- **Media rights** - the checkbox "I own or have the rights to every image, recording and music in this video" (`:275`), enabled only on an `assembled` package whose rights are not confirmed; ticking it opens "Confirm the media rights?", an alert dialog with Cancel focused, and only "Confirm the rights" sends the project id to `scout:operator.mediaRights`; Cancel, Esc or the backdrop send nothing and leave it unticked. Once confirmed it stays ticked and disabled, with "Confirmed <when>. This cannot be undone.", or "Confirmed. This cannot be undone." for a package with no `rightsConfirmedAt` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:492`); a failed confirmation unticks it and shows the reason (`:914`).
- **Approve package** (`:305`) - disabled while any reason holds, the reasons read above it as one warning line, "Before approving: " and the reasons, the first lowercased (`:296`, built at `:819`): "The video is not assembled.", "Confirm the media rights first.", "<n> check failed." or "<n> checks failed.", "1 claim is not supported or waived." or "<n> claims are not supported or waived.". Otherwise it sends the project id to `scout:operator.packageApproval`; an approved package shows "Approved <when>" (`:289`), and no button. A refusal shows the control plane's reason.

The page loads the list on open and every 10 s while it is open (`:24`, `:969`); a failed load shows the error above the picker and keeps the last list.

In main, `scout:operator.assembly`, `scout:operator.mediaRights` and `scout:operator.packageApproval` take a project id, trimmed and required (`ui/packages/main/src/ipc/operator.ts:813`); `scout:operator.packageMetadata` (`:888`) trims the title, the description and each tag, drops empty tags, and refuses, naming every limit broken at once with the control plane's words, a title outside 1 to 100 characters, a description over 5000, a tag over 100 and tags over 500 together, counted YouTube's way, two more for a tag with a space and a comma between tags (`:280`, `:287`). A refusal reaches the renderer as `operator/invalid-request`. The client calls `AssembleProject`, `ConfirmMediaRights`, `EditPackageMetadata` and `ApprovePackage` (`ui/packages/main/src/control-plane/client.ts:1055`) with the operator error mapping above; `toPackage` (`:714`) maps the package, an absent package is `null` (`:618`) and so are its absent video file, thumbnail, approval time and error. The DTOs are `VideoPackageDto`, `ShortClipDto`, `PackageCheckDto` and `PackageMetadataRequest` (`ui/packages/shared/src/ipc-contract.ts:1026`, `:1047`, `:1055`, `:1062`). `scout-media` serves `.mp4` as `video/mp4` (`ui/packages/main/src/operator/media.ts:29`) under the same confinement, streaming and byte ranges as every preview; the thumbnail's png or jpg was already served. The owner cannot attach an mp4: the attach rules are unchanged.

### Publish

- **Publish** (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/assembly/assembly.component.ts:321`) - shown on an approved package while the project has no publication, or one that `failed` without a video id (`:838`); it opens the publish dialog (`:369`), a `MatDialog` on the theme's card, whose fields start empty on every open (`:846`).
- **Dialog.** "Who can watch it" is a required choice of Public, Unlisted or Private, each with its line, none chosen at first (`:379`). "Publish at (London time)" is a `datetime-local` read as Europe/London, with the line "Optional, only with Private: YouTube makes it public at that time.", enabled only with private; choosing public or unlisted clears it (`:392`, `:857`). The time is converted to ISO UTC by `londonToUtcIso` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/operator/london.ts:23`), which finds London's offset with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` and checks it once more, so a summer time is one hour earlier in UTC; the page shows "Publishes <time> Europe/London (<ISO> UTC)." "Made for kids" is a required yes or no (`:401`). "Contains realistic synthetic or altered media" is pre-ticked when any scene's narration or picture is `generated`, and the owner can change it (`:421`, `:827`). The dialog lists what will run (`:428`): "Upload the video "<title>" as <Public, Unlisted or Private>", "Set the thumbnail shown above (<file>)" when the package has one, and "Schedule: public at <time> Europe/London" with a schedule. "Confirm and publish" stays disabled while any reason holds (`:737`): "Choose public, private or unlisted.", "Say whether the video is made for kids.", "Enter a date and a time.", "The scheduled time must be in the future." It sends `scout:operator.publication` (`:843`) and closes; a refusal keeps it open with the control plane's reason.
- **Publication panel** (`:322`) - the publication's status, the video link `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>` once there is a video id, opened through `scout:app.openExternal`, whose allow-list has `www.youtube.com` (`:863`), the scheduled time in London, and the publication error. A project with a publication stays in the picker whatever its status (`:955`).

In main, `scout:operator.publication` (`ui/packages/main/src/ipc/operator.ts:825`) refuses before the control plane, through `validPublication` (`:311`): a privacy other than public, private or unlisted ("Choose public, private or unlisted."), a made-for-kids or synthetic-media value that is not a boolean (never defaulted), a schedule with public or unlisted ("A scheduled time needs private; YouTube makes the video public at that time."), one that is not an ISO UTC time (`:132`) and one that is not in the future. `scout:operator.proposalDecision` (`:844`) needs a boolean `accept`; `scout:operator.experiment` (`:863`) needs whole arm hours from 24 to 168 ("Each arm runs 24 to 168 hours."); `scout:operator.experimentAdoption` (`:820`) takes a project id. The client sends `PublishPackage`, `DecideProposal`, `StartExperiment` and `AdoptExperimentWinner` (`ui/packages/main/src/control-plane/client.ts:1060`) and maps the publication with `toPublication` (`:653`): its snapshots, retention points, proposals and experiment, each absent optional field as `null`. The DTOs are `PublicationDto`, `SnapshotDto`, `ProposalDto`, `ExperimentDto`, `PublishPackageRequest`, `ProposalDecisionRequest` and `ExperimentStartRequest` (`ui/packages/shared/src/ipc-contract.ts:879`, `:893`, `:915`, `:924`, `:944`, `:952`, `:958`).
