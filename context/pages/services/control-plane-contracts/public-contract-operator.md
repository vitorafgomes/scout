A new assembly clears the errors, the files, the checks, `rights_confirmed` and `approved_at`, and keeps the metadata only when `metadata_edited`.
  - `EditPackageMetadata` takes `EditPackageMetadataRequest` (`:336`: `project_id`, `title`, `description`, `tags`): the title 1 to 100 characters, the description at most 5000, each tag 1 to 100 and the tags at most 500 together, after trimming, else `INVALID_ARGUMENT`; on an `assembled` or `approved` project only, it sets `metadata_edited`, and an approved package goes back to `assembled` without `approved_at`. `ConfirmMediaRights` takes `GetProjectRequest` and sets `rights_confirmed` on an `assembled` project only. Neither needs the grant.
  - `ApprovePackage` takes `GetProjectRequest` and answers the `Project` in `approved` with `approved_at`. It needs the grant; a project that is not `assembled`, rights not confirmed, a check that failed, or a claim neither `supported` nor `waived` is `INVALID_ARGUMENT` with a sentence per cause that names the count. The approval is one write-log entry, outcome `approved package`, approved by `owner`.
  - `Publication` (`src/Scout.ControlPlane.Contracts/protos/operator.proto:354`): `status` (`publish_approved` set by the owner; `uploading`, `uploaded`, `scheduled`, `published`, `failed` by the worker), the optional `video_id`, set once by the worker, `privacy_status` (`public`, `private`, `unlisted`), the optional ISO 8601 `publish_at`, only with `private`, `made_for_kids`, `synthetic_media`, the optional `uploaded_at` and `error`, `snapshots`, `proposals` and the optional `experiment`. The owner sets the privacy, the publish time and the two declarations; the worker sends them back unchanged (`FAILED_PRECONDITION` otherwise).
  - `Snapshot` (`:357`): a video's numbers over one `window` (`24h`, `7d`), `taken_at`, views, minutes watched, average view duration and percentage, subscribers gained and lost, likes, comments, shares, the optional `impressions` and `impressions_ctr`, unset without a reach report, and `retention` (`RetentionPoint`, `:374`: `elapsed_ratio`, `audience_watch_ratio`). A snapshot the worker sends replaces the stored one of its window.
  - `Proposal` (`:379`): `id`, `kind` (`title`, `thumbnail`, `topic`, `strategy`), `text`, `reason`, `status` (`proposed` from the worker; `accepted` or `rejected` from the owner; `tested` once an experiment used it) and the optional `thumbnail_file`, required for a thumbnail proposal.
  - `Experiment` (`:388`): `id`, `kind` (`title` or `thumbnail`), `status` (`running` from the owner's start; `concluded` or `inconclusive` from the worker, or `inconclusive` when the owner revokes YouTube; `adopted` once the owner adopted its winner), `arms` (`ExperimentArm`, `:397`: `id` `control`, `variant-a`, `variant-b`, `label`, `value` the title or thumbnail file, the optional ISO 8601 `started_at` and `ended_at`, `impressions`, `clicks`, `average_view_percentage`), the optional `winner_arm_id` and `result`. How long each arm runs is kept by the control plane and is not on the wire.
  - `PublishPackage` takes `PublishPackageRequest` (`:408`: `project_id`, `privacy_status`, optional `publish_at` in the future, `made_for_kids`, `synthetic_media`) and answers the `Project` with its publication `publish_approved`; it needs the grant, an `approved` package, no video on YouTube, a publication that is unset or `failed`, and no YouTube action of the project running; it rejects the project's earlier approved actions as superseded and queues the upload and the thumbnail as actions the owner approved. `DecideProposal` takes `DecideProposalRequest` (`:416`: `project_id`, `proposal_id`, `accept`) and decides a `proposed` proposal once, no grant. `StartExperiment` takes `StartExperimentRequest` (`:422`: `project_id`, `arm_hours` 24 to 168, 0 meaning 48) and `AdoptExperimentWinner` takes `GetProjectRequest`; both need the grant and queue approved actions. Every rule and sentence is on [[pages/services/control-plane]]; a broken rule is `INVALID_ARGUMENT`.
  - `AudienceComment` (`:444`): one top-level comment on the owner's channel: `id` (the top-level comment's YouTube id, `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,100}$`), `thread_id`, `video_id`, `video_title`, `author` (the display name only), `text` (plain text, at most 2000 characters), ISO 8601 `published_at`, `like_count`, `kind` (`question`, `request`, `praise`, `feedback`, `other`, `spam`), `theme` (at most 40 characters), `status` (`new`, `draft_requested`, `drafted`, `reply_approved`, `replying`, `replied`, `quarantined`, `hold_approved`, `held`, `dismissed`, `failed`), `draft` (at most 1000), the optional `approved_text` (only the owner sets it; the worker posts exactly it), `reply_id`, `error` and `decided_at`, and `fetched_at`, which the control plane stamps when it first stores the comment and ignores from a sender. `AudienceIdea` (`:464`): `id` (`idea-<comment id>`), `text` (at most 200), `comment_id`, `status` (`proposed`, `used`, `dismissed`) and the optional `project_id`. `CommentsView` (`:472`): `comments`, newest first, `ideas`, and the optional `synced_at`, `sync_requested_at` and `sync_error`.
  - `ListComments` takes the empty `ListCommentsRequest` (`:427`) and needs no grant. `RequestCommentSync` takes the same request and stamps `sync_requested_at` under the grant. `DecideComment` takes `DecideCommentRequest` (`:429`: `comment_id`, `decision` `draft`, `dismiss`, `not_spam` or `hold`); `draft` and `hold` need the grant, `hold` queues one approved `comments.setModerationStatus` action, and `dismiss` rejects the comment's approved reply or hold and is refused while its action is executing or it is `replying` with a live action; neither `draft` nor `ApproveReply` accepts a comment that already has a reply on YouTube. `ApproveReply` takes `ApproveReplyRequest` (`:434`: `comment_id`, `text`, 1 to 1000 characters after trimming, no control character but a new line) under the grant and queues one approved `comments.insert` action. `DecideIdea` takes `DecideIdeaRequest` (`:439`: `idea_id`, `use`); `use` starts a project on the idea's text under the grant.
