## Meetings

The Meetings screen (route `meetings`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/post-connect-shell.component.ts:35`) lists the meetings the owner kept from the quick-ask window's Stop row ([[pages/services/desktop-quick-ask]], Meeting mode; [[pages/decisions/0037-meetings-kept-locally-in-sqlite]], amendment "desktop side"); the worker keeps them ([[pages/services/meetings]]).

- **List** (`MeetingsComponent`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/meetings/meetings.component.ts:315`): the lede "The meetings you kept from the quick-ask window. They stay on this computer." (`:44`), `scout:local.meetings` with `limit` 100 on open (`:21`, `:393`), sorted newest first by `startedAt` in the renderer (`:395`). Until the list answers, a progress bar sits over three skeleton rows, each a card with two bars, hidden from screen readers under one "Loading the meetings" status (`:74`, `:82`); the first read is the only one that shows it, and a later read keeps the rows. Each meeting is a row card (`data-test="meetings-list"`, `:100`) with its title, `yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm` start, duration in whole minutes or "under a minute" (`:337`), its line and question counts, and "Export as Markdown" and Delete, each with its icon; an export's outcome, "Saved", "Not saved" or the refusal's sentence, is a second line under the counts (`:110`), and a saved export is also told in the notice "Saved" with OK (`:351`). Delete all sits at the right of the header while a meeting is kept (`:47`). Empty: "No meetings kept yet. Start one from Meeting in the quick-ask tools and tick Keep when you finish." (`:93`). A refused list shows its sentence in an alert with Try again (`:63`, `:64`), such as the worker's not-configured one; when a later read is refused the alert sits over the rows already listed. Delete and Delete all ask first through the confirmation dialog with Cancel focused, "Delete this meeting?" or "Delete all meetings?" (`:361`); while a delete runs every row's Delete is disabled (`:128`), and a refused delete shows its sentence on the page (`:71`). Once main has answered a delete, the notice "Meeting deleted" or "All meetings deleted" with OK says so before the list is read again (`:381`). The notices are the shared `NoticeService`'s, success notices at the bottom centre of the window for its default four seconds, not kept in the bell ([[pages/decisions/0041-angular-material-in-the-renderer]]); a cancelled or failed export and a refused or cancelled delete show none. The approved artboard's transcript pane was dropped on 2026-11-07: no channel reads a kept meeting's transcript into the renderer. Export, Delete and Delete all, and the four meeting requests in main, are described on [[pages/services/desktop-quick-ask]], Kept meetings.
- **Look** (the `Meetings.dc.html` artboard, version 10 of [[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]]): the page sets 14 px text and starts at the left edge of the pane, at most 1200 px wide (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/meetings/meetings.component.ts:148`, `:157`). For its outlined buttons it sets three of Material's button tokens: 36 px high, 14 px of padding and a regular-weight label (`:159` to `:161`); the label and outline colours are not the page's, they come from the shell's stylesheet, and Delete and Delete all alone take `--sc-error` (`:190`). The icon sits 8 px before the text, centred on it (`:185`). The alert's sentence is `--sc-fg` on the error container with the icon in `--sc-error` and Try again on `--sc-surface` (`:192`, `:203`, `:207`); the progress bar's track is `--sc-surface-2` (`:210`). Cards keep `--sc-radius`, 12 px, where the artboard draws 14 px. The artboard has a preview state for each state the screen reaches: Loaded, Loading, Empty, Error, Error with meetings kept, the two dialogs, Export results, Exporting, Deleting and Delete failed. Its notice after an export or a delete is a design the screen does not have yet.
- **The database path.** `buildEnv` always sets `SCOUT__MEETINGS__DBPATH` to `<userData>/meetings.db`, empty without the folder (`ui/packages/main/src/worker/build-env.ts:126`, wired at `ui/packages/main/src/main.ts:847`), and the supervisor never passes an inherited value of it in any spelling (`ui/packages/main/src/worker/supervisor.ts:479`).

### Long lists on Features

The owner, 2026-11-07, translated: "there has to be some way to hide the projects; everything is open and that makes the layout ugly. There should be a place to expand, and to hide again on a second click." Every list on Features that can grow with the owner's account sits in `sc-collapsible-list` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/collapsible-list.component.ts:210`): a `button` header with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` and a one-line summary, collapsed by default (`:230`), opened by a click and closed by a second one. Each list's choice is remembered under its key in `localStorage` `scout:features.lists` (`:17`); a list given `startOpen` (`:220`) is open each time it is drawn instead, keeps a close only while it stays drawn, stores nothing and forgets a choice an earlier version stored under its key (`:241`, `:41`): the Kubernetes contexts list, because the owner read the folded list as no configuration found, read and written behind try and catch so a storage that refuses falls back to collapsed (`:27`). The filter field sits above the rows whenever the list is open, as `Features.dc.html` draws it (until 2026-11-10 it showed only past 8 rows); past 8 rows the list scrolls inside a 256 px height (`:198`), unless the panel sets `--sc-list-max-height`, as the Kubernetes contexts list does (see Kubernetes connection). Today two lists use it: the Scout bot's repositories in the GitHub panel, "Repositories: N (<login> can post in these)" (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/github-connect.component.ts:910`), and the detected Kubernetes contexts (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/kubernetes-connect.component.ts:95`). The Docker panel keeps its own remembered sections. Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/collapsible-list.component.spec.ts:59` (collapsed at first, a click opens and a second closes, remembered across a remount, the storage failure, the filter, the capped height), the summaries in `github-connect.component.spec.ts` and `kubernetes-connect.component.spec.ts`, which also holds the contexts open at first, the stored closed choice forgotten and a close kept across closing and reopening the panel (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/features/kubernetes-connect.component.spec.ts:193` to `:228`).
