## Discord setup

**The setup frame and its steps follow the artboard** (`context/prototypes/desktop/Wizard.dc.html`; version 4 of [[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]]). Before setup the wizard frame draws a header bar (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/shell.component.ts:42`): the Scout mark, "Scout setup", the step count and the same theme switch as the top bar (Light, Dark and System through `ThemeStore`, `:50`), above the stepper. The stepper (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/step-indicator.component.ts:33`) shows a done step with a check on the ok container, the current step as a pill with its number on a filled accent circle, the steps to come numbered in an outlined circle, and a dash after every step but the last; its steps are not links. Every step is one centred column 760 px wide, a title with its lede and one card, from `WIZARD_PAGE_STYLES` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/wizard-footer.component.ts:10`), which also carries the alert box and paints a filled button `--sc-accent-strong` under `--sc-on-accent`. Every text button of the steps and the footer is a `matButton` ([[pages/decisions/0041-angular-material-in-the-renderer]]): Back and a secondary Continue outlined, a main or blocked Continue filled (`:112`). The exceptions are plain buttons: the three theme options of the header, the Download Discord link of the Discord card (a card's choice is a radio input in a label), the eye of the token field and the server rows of Add to server.

- **Create bot:** the three portal steps and the warning "Reset Token stops the old token working. If this bot already runs somewhere else, that copy goes offline." (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/provision/provision.component.ts:32`). A portal that did not open shows "The Developer Portal did not open." with the address and Copy address, and no longer the raw failure (`:60`); main logs that failure with the host and its reason, any address in the reason replaced by `[address]`, because the reason comes from the operating system and may repeat the address (`ui/packages/main/src/ipc/app.ts:72`; the two rows "a browser that cannot be opened (...) is logged with its reason and the host, never the address, and the window is still answered with the failure", `ui/packages/main/test/ipc-app.test.ts`).

- **Bot token:** the field with an eye button named Show token or Hide token (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/token-paste/token-paste.component.ts:61`), which shows the token in the field and nowhere else on the page and which a save turns back to hidden, whatever Discord answers (`submit`, `:416`), a help line that main's error replaces, a save button reading Save, Checking..., Saved or Try again (`saveLabel`, `:374`), and the checks as rows. When Message Content Intent needs the portal and Open Developer Portal cannot open the browser, the check row says "The Developer Portal did not open. Open this address in any browser, then come back and check again:" with the address, and the token field keeps its own state (`portalFailed`, `:366`, `openPortal`, `:467`). Its last line reads "The token is kept encrypted on this computer, under a key from its keyring." (`:208`), which is what the vault does (`ui/packages/main/src/vault/safe-storage-vault.ts`: an encrypted file, the key in the keyring).

- **Add to server:** the invite button, then the waiting line with a spinner, then "Scout joined" with the server on the ok banner (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/oauth-install/oauth-install.component.ts:36`); "Scout is already in these servers" lists one row per server (`:68`).

- **Launch:** "Start Scout" with a chip (Starting Scout, Scout is running, Could not start; `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/worker-launch/worker-launch.component.ts:37`), a progress bar while starting, the rows Chat app, AI Source and Bot token (`:66`), "Scout stopped before it was ready." with one plain sentence, and Show details, open on a failure (`:87`), with the log. A start main refuses is read with `readIpcError`: a coded refusal shows its message, any other shows "Scout could not start. The reason is under Show details.", and what main answered is printed under Show details, above the log, never in the failure box (`startWorker`, `:279`). Retry and Cancel stay as they were.

- **Ready:** "You are all set", then "Setup is complete" with one line saying whether Scout runs, in which server and with which AI Source (`summary`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/ready/ready.component.ts:354`; no AI Source is named until a model is chosen, so the line never names one above the no-model banner), the no-model banner, and "Scout process and start over", a section closed while Scout runs and open otherwise until the owner opens or closes it, after which it stays as they left it whatever the worker does (`:82`, `processOpen`, `:340`): the state chip (Running, Starting on the artboard's surface and accent pair, Stopped, Crashed, Idle; `:30`), PID, uptime and restarts, Stop, Restart or Start, the last 200 log lines with Copy, and "Start over with another bot" with Start over.

- **In the artboard and not built**, each being new behaviour: done steps as links back, the waiting timer and "Open the invite again", member counts, Launch's chip details ("Connected to Discord.", "Stopped after 6 seconds."), its Back and Continue footer and "Change in AI Sources", Ready's "What to do next" cards, Back and "Open the chat". The artboard has the code's six steps and no Welcome step.

- **Tests:** the "as in the prototype" tables in each step's spec under `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/shell.component.spec.ts` ("The setup header as in the prototype: Scout setup, the step count and the theme switch") and `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/step-indicator.component.spec.ts` ("The step indicator as in the prototype: done, current and to-do steps").
