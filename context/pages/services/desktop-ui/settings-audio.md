## Settings

- **Audio** (`AudioSettingsComponent`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/settings/audio-settings.component.ts:239`, in the second column between "Floating button and quick ask" and Desktop): "The microphone Scout listens with, for dictation, conversation and meetings, and the speaker it reads answers aloud on." A Microphone picker lists System default first, then every audio input the system lists, an unlabelled one as "Microphone 1", "Microphone 2" by its place; the `default` and `communications` entries and inputs without an id are left out (`AudioDevices.list`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-devices.ts:107`). A Speaker picker lists the outputs the same way and shows only where an AudioContext has `setSinkId` (`speakerSelectable`, `:99`). Opening Settings never opens the microphone: before the first capture the system may list devices without names or ids, so only System default is offered, with "Press and hold Test once so Scout can list your microphones and speakers." under the Microphone picker while any audio device is listed without an id (`hidden`, gone once the system lists them), and the list is read again after a Test, on a `devicechange` and each time Settings opens. A choice is saved at once in renderer storage, `scout:audio-input` and `scout:audio-output`, empty for System default; a saved device the system does not list stays selected as "The chosen microphone" or "The chosen speaker". These are desktop-only and not secret, so they are not vault keys ([[pages/decisions/0050-read-aloud-and-conversation-mode]], amendment "the microphone and the speaker"). How each capture and read aloud use them, and the fallback notice when one is unplugged, are on [[pages/services/desktop-quick-ask]] (Microphone and speaker). **Test** is held, by pointer, Space or Enter (a key's auto-repeat never starts it again, so a held key cannot keep the microphone open past the limit), and released by the same, by leaving the button or the page, or after 10 s (`MicrophoneTest`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/level-meter.ts:51`, `MICROPHONE_TEST_LIMIT_MS`, `:13`): it opens the chosen microphone with the conversation's constraints, automatic gain off, so the room is not raised, on its own recorder, so a dictation is never cut, and shows a `role="meter"` bar, "Microphone level", of the RMS per 20 ms frame, rising at once and falling by 0.85 per frame, on a square-root scale to 0.2. The first second of sound, 50 frames with digital silence left out, sets the room (the grey mark), and the speech mark, in the text colour, is the worker's threshold over it, max(0.015, 3 x the room counted as at most 0.05), the constants `RmsThreshold`, `RoomFactor` and `RoomCap` of `src/Scout.Transcription.Whisper/LiveUtteranceSegmenter.cs:23` (`speechThreshold`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/level-meter.ts:23`; a spec reads the C# file and fails when they differ). On release the recorder stops, so the tracks stop and the microphone is released. A Test whose microphone is refused, missing or busy shows that sentence in the section, a list that fails "Scout could not list the audio devices.", each as an alert. Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/settings/audio-settings.component.spec.ts`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/level-meter.spec.ts`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/speech/audio-devices.spec.ts`, and the order of the sections in `settings.component.spec.ts`. Prototype: the Audio card of `context/prototypes/desktop/Settings.dc.html`.

- **Appearance** (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/settings/settings.component.ts:107`): Light, Dark and System, each with its icon and the same labels as the top bar's switch, as 34 px pills in a segmented control over "The same choice as the switch in the top bar. System follows your desktop's light or dark setting, in every Scout window." (`:126`), bound to the same `ThemeStore`; the pressed one is main's answer, and a refusal keeps it and shows "Scout could not use that theme." in the card. It replaced the "Theme - Coming soon" card.

### Logs screen

The Logs screen (route `logs`, in the menu when Settings shows it) fills the shell's content area: its host and page are `height: 100%` with the page's overflow hidden, and only the log viewer scrolls (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/log-viewer/logs.component.ts:250`, `:263`); the page sets its text at 14 px and is at most 1200 px wide, and the viewer is as tall as its lines until they fill the page, as `context/prototypes/desktop/Logs.dc.html` draws it ([[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]], version 11). It opens with "What the Scout worker writes while it runs. Lines stay in this window until you clear them." (`:88`) and Pause or Resume (pressed while paused), Copy all and Clear, each with its icon, 36 px pills with the icon 8 px before the label, the size the page sets on Material's button tokens (`:258`). The levels are four pressed switches in a group named "Show levels", Info, Warning, Error and Debug, each with the artboard's glyph (`:33`) and how many lines it holds (`:121`, `:528`), beside "Search the log", which keeps the lines that contain it in any case (`:156`). A line's level comes from the console formatter's prefix (`info:`, `warn:`, `fail:`, `crit:`, `dbug:`, `trce:`) when it has one, so `info: Scout.Knowledge.FailoverPolicy[0]` is info; only a line without a prefix falls back to the words it contains, and an indented continuation takes its header's level (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/log-level.ts:22`). Under them the worker chip reads "Worker running", "Worker starting" or "Worker stopped" from `LogStore.status`, seeded on open with `scout:worker.status` while the store has heard no state event yet (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/log-viewer/logs.component.ts:579`, `:608`), with "Following new lines", "Paused: new lines are kept and shown when you resume" or "No new lines while the worker is stopped" (`:594`), and "N errors in this session". **Lines written before the screen opened.** Main keeps the worker's last 2,000 lines once each in the supervisor's ring; `LogStore`, created when a screen first needs it and again after a reload, follows the pushed `scout:worker.events` and then asks main for that ring over `scout:worker.logSnapshot` (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/log.store.ts:41`, `ui/packages/main/src/ipc/worker.ts:92`), leaving out the ones written before the owner's last Clear and putting the rest before the ones that arrived meanwhile without doubling them; when main cannot answer, the store keeps following new lines. **Following.** While the owner is at the newest line, every change to the lines keeps the newest line in view; once the owner scrolls up the view stays where he put it until he scrolls back to the bottom, and a pause freezes the lines (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/log-viewer/logs.component.ts:565`, `:618`). Each row is tracked by its worker event, so when the 2,000-line window drops its oldest line the rows of the lines that stay are kept (`:195`). A row shows the local time of day the line was written, `14:02:11.384`, in a `time` element whose `datetime` is the whole ISO date and whose title and accessible description are the whole local date and time (`2026-11-10 23:59:59.900`), so lines either side of midnight can be told apart, all worked out once per line (`:60`, `:197`); a timestamp that cannot be read shows as written, then the level in 64 px and the worker's text as text, never markup, wrapping under a narrow window and keeping its indentation (`:425`). The viewer sits on `--sc-code-bg` and an error line on the error container (`:431`); it is a `role="log"` with `aria-live="off"`, as the prototype has it, so a screen reader is not read every line while the worker writes, and the error count beside the worker chip is the polite live region (`:189`, `:181`). Three empty states (`:213`, `:227`, `:235`): "No line matches the search and the levels you chose. N lines are hidden." with "Show every line"; "Scout is running and has not written anything yet. New lines appear here as it works." with Open Settings; and "Scout is not running, so it writes nothing. Start it to see its log here." with Start Scout, which calls `scout:worker.start` and toasts "Could not start Scout: <reason>" when main refuses (`:687`); Nothing matches carries the artboard's funnel, an icon of the registry (`:212`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/icon.component.ts:49`). Every notice of the screen has the artboard's OK (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/log-viewer/logs.component.ts:40`). Copy all copies what the screen shows, as the level switches, the search and a pause leave it, with the level each line shows: "Copied N lines" through `NoticeService` when every line held is shown, "Copied N of M lines" when some are hidden (M counts the lines held on screen, the frozen ones while paused), "Nothing to copy" when nothing is shown, or "Could not copy the logs: <reason>" when the clipboard refuses (`:648`); the copied lines carry the whole ISO date the screen keeps in each row's `datetime`. Clear asks first (`:670`) in the shared confirmation, the artboards' dialog card: "Clear the log?", 440 px wide and 120 px from the top on `--sc-scrim`, Cancel focused, Clear white on `--sc-danger` with the trash glyph (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/confirm-dialog.component.ts:112`), and then shows "Log cleared" (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/wizard/log-viewer/logs.component.ts:684`); what it cleared stays cleared after a reload of the window, as the screen's copy says: Clear keeps the time it ran in the window's `localStorage` (`scout:logs-cleared-at`) and a store created later leaves out every line of main's ring written up to that time, while the lines written after it come back (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/log.store.ts:63`, `:41`); when the window's storage is unavailable Clear still empties the screen and does not throw, and only then do the cleared lines come back after a reload; `LogStore.copy` writes the lines it is given, or every line held when given none, as the Ready step's copy does, resolves with how many it copied and rejects with the clipboard error (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/log.store.ts:82`).
