## How the rules are kept

- [[pages/invariants/no-executive-writes]]: start, stop, pull and delete mutate the host; since 2026-11-11 they are ruled a reviewed local write on the owner's click, outside the feature grant, reached by the desktop token only, with the download's and the delete's log lines above ([[pages/decisions/0032-per-feature-write-permissions]], amendment of 2026-11-11). The GitHub catalog entry lists only the read-only GitHub App permissions Metadata, Contents, Pull requests and Issues, each `ScopeAccess.Read` (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:48`, guarded by `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:24`), and its setup steps describe "Connect with GitHub" ([[pages/decisions/0019-github-app-device-flow]]). Since 2026-11-12 every row of the GitHub, Kubernetes, YouTube and Desktop entries carries the approved artboard's short sentence as its `Summary`, or, where the artboard's sentence left out an action the row declares or said something untrue, a sentence in the same style that covers all of it, written into the artboard too; the GitHub, YouTube and Desktop descriptions are the artboard's; and the GitHub entry's fifth read row, `Notifications`, "Search every five minutes for your mentions, review requests and replies; read the pull requests that mention your Scout bot and the bot's own name" (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:80`), is drawn on the artboard as well; the GitHub `Pull request verdicts` actions gained a verdict asked in chat and `New issues` the issues a findings card opens on Open issues, so the Allow writes confirmation names every path its summary names (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:107`, `:121`; `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:77`, `:189`, `:246`, `:325`; every write action of GitHub and YouTube kept in full, `:100`, `:272`; the summary over gRPC, `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Integration/FeatureWritesGrpcTests.cs:28`). The sentences written for this, and why, are listed on [[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]] (Features, K2). The `pat` credential field stays for a host without the desktop sign-in. The Kubernetes entry says it reads pods, deployments and events using the Kubernetes configuration on this computer, with consent, read-only (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:157`, guarded by `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:166`). Since 2026-11-12 it declares four read rows, under `WriteClass.None`, in the order and with the words of the approved artboard: `Namespaces`, "List the namespaces of each context you chose"; `Pods`, "Read pod status and restarts; never logs or secrets"; `Deployments`, "Read replicas and rollout status"; `Events`, "Read recent events" (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:168`, guarded by `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:175` and, over gRPC, `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Integration/FeatureWritesGrpcTests.cs:56`). They are the reads of the Kubernetes plugin's functions ([[pages/services/plugin-kubernetes]]). Its setup steps name `KUBECONFIG` and `~/.kube/config`, recommend a read-only user, and no longer promise a keyring or ask for a pasted kubeconfig (`tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:200`). It asks for no credentials (`tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:212`): the desktop card reads the local kubeconfig, and a host without the desktop configures `Scout:Plugins:Kubernetes` ([[pages/decisions/0020-kubernetes-from-local-kubeconfig]]). The YouTube entry is the channel operator's contract under `WriteClass.Executive` (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:197`, guarded by `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:219`): reads `channels.list`, `playlistItems.list`, `videos.list`, `search.list`, `commentThreads.list`, `youtubeAnalytics reports.query` and `youtubeReporting reports.list`, the reach reports, the only source of thumbnail impressions and click-through rate (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:247`); `Write` rows for scripts, narration, images, the scene list, the video and thumbnails, and the recording the owner attaches and its transcript, under Scout's data folder and for `youtubeReporting jobs.create`, the reach reporting job created once (`:262`); `IrreversibleWrite` rows `videos.insert`, upload as public, private or unlisted or as private with a publish time (`:267`), `videos.update`, `thumbnails.set`, `playlistItems.insert`, `comments.insert` and `comments.setModerationStatus`. Its description is the artboard's: it reads a channel's overview, uploads, statistics, search results and comments, and with the write permission produces videos under Scout's folder and publishes what the owner approves; the earlier line saying replying to and moderating comments were not implemented was dropped on 2026-11-12 because both run (a reply, `comments.insert`, and a hold for review, `comments.setModerationStatus`, executed by the channel operator after the owner's approval). The same day `videos.update` was reworded to what Scout does with it, a title test's title changes, and `comments.setModerationStatus` to a hold for review, never a publish or a reject (`tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:246`, `:272`). It asks for one `api_key` field whose help points to the Google Cloud Console; its setup steps offer the key or the sign-in from the Scout app (`tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:288`, [[pages/services/plugin-youtube]]). The Desktop entry declares what the desktop itself captures, under `WriteClass.None`, with no credential or settings field and no setup steps (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/FeatureCatalog.cs:304`, guarded by `tests/Scout.ControlPlane.Tests/Features/FeatureCatalogTests.cs:305`): the read rows `Screen`, "Send one screenshot with a question when you attach it", `Microphone`, "Record a voice question", "Listen while you speak" and, since 2026-11-12, "Listen while you record a meeting", and `System audio`, "Listen to what this computer plays while you keep System audio on" and "Listen to what this computer plays while you record a meeting"; the `Screen` row also lists "Read the text on a screenshot with the bundled OCR program" ([[pages/decisions/0035-screenshot-attached-to-a-question]], [[pages/decisions/0036-text-read-from-a-screenshot-by-a-local-ocr-program]], [[pages/decisions/0034-live-transcription-on-the-local-adapter]]). It is informational: nothing gates the local adapter on it, its snapshot stays `Disabled` with the no-op activator, and the desktop card shows it as Connected with its Reads and no toggle, credentials or write grant.

- [[pages/invariants/secrets-never-in-source]]: credentials go to `ICredentialStore` (in memory by default), not to the JSON state file, whose record holds only `Enabled`, `LastError`, `Settings`, `Granted` and `GrantedAt` (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Features/IFeatureStateStore.cs:11`); the queue and the log hold no secret either.

- [[pages/invariants/local-first-default]]: the default endpoint is localhost (`src/Scout.ControlPlane/Providers/OllamaProviderOptions.cs:12`).
