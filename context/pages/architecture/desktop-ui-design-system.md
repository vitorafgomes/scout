## Theme tokens

The parts above are the 2026-04 design; what ships is `ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss` ([[pages/decisions/0041-angular-material-in-the-renderer]], [[pages/decisions/0045-theme-and-notification-center]]). The page carries `data-theme="light"` or `"dark"` on `html`, set by the theme store from main's answer; until it answers, `prefers-color-scheme` picks the same scheme, because main's `nativeTheme.themeSource` drives it.

- **Material roles, per scheme** (`html[data-theme=...]`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss:115`, `:120`): surface, surface-container, surface-container-high, outline-variant, on-surface, on-surface-variant, outline, primary (accent text and links: #2a56c6 light, #8fb2f9 dark), on-primary, primary-container (the filled button: #3466e8 in both schemes, white text at 4.99:1), on-primary-container, error (#b3261e light, #f28b82 dark, so error text reads on the dark surfaces), on-error.
- **Aliases on `:root`** (`:140`), the same in both schemes because they point at the roles: `--sc-bg`, `--sc-surface`, `--sc-surface-2`, `--sc-line`, `--sc-fg`, `--sc-fg-2`, `--sc-muted`, `--sc-accent`, `--sc-accent-strong`, `--sc-on-accent`, `--sc-error`, `--sc-on-danger` (white, the text and glyphs on `--sc-danger`: Stop on the Chat screen and in the quick-ask window, and the floating button's failed badge; a literal since 2026-11-10), `--sc-focus` (= accent), `--sc-nav-active` (accent at 16 % over the surface), `--sc-mono`, `--sc-radius` (14 px since 2026-11-10, the corners every artboard draws on a card, an alert or a dialog; the approval card and the Models rows, Checking local and Local LLM not connected keep the 12 px their artboards draw, as values of their own), `--sc-radius-sm`.
- **Literal tokens with a light and a dark value:** `--sc-ok`, `--sc-warn`, the container pairs `--sc-ok-container` / `--sc-on-ok-container`, `--sc-warn-container` / `--sc-on-warn-container`, `--sc-error-container` / `--sc-on-error-container`, `--sc-info-container` / `--sc-on-info-container`, `--sc-unread` (the bell's unread row: #eef3ff light, #17223a dark, as `Main.dc.html` draws it, since 2026-11-12), `--sc-code-bg`, `--sc-media` (the tile behind a video, a scene picture or a picture not made yet: #d9dfeb light, #22304a dark, under `--sc-fg-2`), `--sc-scrim`, `--sc-danger` (the red under white text, the count badge: #b3261e light, #c5372c dark), `--sc-pressed` (the pressed button of a segmented control, #ffffff light, #253249 dark, as every artboard draws it, since 2026-11-10) `--sc-shadow` (the dialog card's shadow, rgba(24, 32, 46, 0.14) light, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45) dark), and the inverse trio of every notice, `--sc-inverse-bg`, `--sc-inverse-fg` and `--sc-inverse-action` (#2a3243, #f5f7fb, #a9c1fa in light; #e6e9f0, #18202e, #2a56c6 in dark, as the Components artboard draws its snackbar). `--sc-pressed` paints the pressed option of Settings' segments, the Models filter, the top bar's theme switch and the setup wizard header's theme switch; quick ask draws the same colours on its own.
- **Contrast:** every text colour reaches 4.5:1 on the background it is defined for, in both schemes; `ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.spec.ts` computes the ratios from the compiled stylesheet.
- **Dark canvas:** `.sc-dark-canvas` (`:125`) pins the dark roles, the dark literals and `color-scheme: dark`, and redeclares the aliases, so the screens not yet on the tokens keep their dark look in the light theme; a screen's migration removes the class from its area. The quick-ask window left the pin on 2026-11-07 and follows the theme through the tokens.
- **Which screens follow the theme (2026-11-07):** Chat (with the turns, the rendered replies and the review findings and verdict cards it renders), AI Sources (with the Ollama models panel), Models (its three tabs, the Whisper models of Transcription among them), Settings (with its Chat connections cards, their modal and the worker card), the five operator pages (Production, Assembly, Comments, Reports, Experiments) and the setup wizard (Welcome with its Discord card, Create bot, Bot token, Add to server, Launch, Ready and the step indicator) paint only with the tokens. Each frame decides the pin per route: the main window's shell drops the dark canvas on `/chat`, `/sources`, `/sources/local-models` (which redirects to `/llm?tab=transcription`), `/llm`, `/settings`, `/features`, `/review`, `/meetings`, `/logs` and the wizard pages, which render inside it once setup has a bot (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/post-connect-shell.component.ts:42`); the operator window's on its five pages, keeping Approvals pinned (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/operator/operator-shell.component.ts:49`); the wizard frame before setup on the wizard pages, keeping a page it can reach before setup, Slack, pinned (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/shell/shell.component.ts:15`). Since the same day Features (with its cards, connect sections, dialogs and the Docker card), Approvals (route `/review`), Meetings and Logs follow it too, so in the main window only Slack before setup and the operator's Approvals page stay on the dark canvas.
- **Token use on the chat:** page `--sc-bg` under `--sc-fg`; the owner's bubble `--sc-accent-strong` under `--sc-on-accent` and Scout's reply a card on `--sc-surface` with a `--sc-line` border, its header with no mark, the name in `--sc-fg` and the time in `--sc-muted`, as the artboards draw them since 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-10; Send and the talk button `--sc-accent-strong` under `--sc-on-accent`, Stop `--sc-danger` under `--sc-on-danger`, the composer a bare row with no strip behind it, Clear `--sc-fg-2` on `--sc-line` and Read answers aloud `--sc-fg` on `--sc-line` in the top bar, the running turn's line `--sc-fg-2`, the field `--sc-bg`; code `--sc-code-bg` in `--sc-mono`; links `--sc-accent`; secondary lines `--sc-muted`; errors `--sc-error`; the severity pills Blocking and Fix on the error and warn container pairs and Suggestion on `--sc-surface-2` under `--sc-fg-2` (since 2026-11-10, the QuickAsk artboard's); a chosen Yes on the ok container under `--sc-on-ok-container` with that colour as its border, and a chosen card's border in it too; a pressed No on the error container with `--sc-error` as its border; Approve, Request changes and the other card buttons transparent under `--sc-fg` on a `--sc-line` border, Post on `--sc-accent-strong` under `--sc-on-accent`; the test chips on the ok, error and warn container pairs, and a run that did not happen on `--sc-surface-2` under `--sc-fg-2`; the GitHub preview alone keeps GitHub's light greys and `color-scheme: light` in either theme, the design approved in [[pages/decisions/0040-review-findings-as-cards-in-chat]].
- **Token use on the operator pages and the wizard:** page text `--sc-fg`, secondary lines `--sc-muted`, cards and dialogs `--sc-surface` with `--sc-line`, inputs `--sc-bg`, the dialog backdrop `--sc-scrim`; filled main actions `--sc-accent-strong` under `--sc-on-accent`, each with its `sc-icon` before the text; a destructive confirm `--sc-error` under `--mat-sys-on-error`; status chips and badges on the ok, warn, error and info container pairs; logs and the URL box `--sc-code-bg`; Assembly's video and Shorts and Production's scene cards on `--sc-media`; the tool buttons over a picture on `--sc-surface`.
- **Typeface** (`ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss:87`, set on `html` at `:112`): one stack, `Inter, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`, feeds Material's plain and brand families and the page's own `font-family`, so an element with no font of its own, such as the top bar's title and source line or the whole notification panel, inherits it instead of Chromium's serif default (Times New Roman before 2026-11-08). The main window, the quick-ask window, the floating button and the operator window all load the same `index.html` (`ui/packages/main/src/browser-window-factory.ts:52`, `ui/packages/main/src/quick-ask/quick-ask.ts:798`, `ui/packages/main/src/operator/operator-window.ts:109`) and so the same stylesheet. Since 2026-11-09 Inter ships inside the app ([[pages/decisions/0041-angular-material-in-the-renderer]], amendment): one `@font-face` (`:245`) loads the variable latin file, weights 100 to 900, from the pinned `@fontsource-variable/inter` package, which the build copies into the renderer output under `media/` with its licence text in `media/inter/LICENSE`; nothing comes from the network and `font-src 'self'` is unchanged. A character outside the latin subset, an italic and any other script fall back to the platform's `sans-serif` (Noto Sans on the Linux machine checked), which until then drew everything. The longer stack in [[pages/architecture/desktop-ui-design-system/2-design-tokens]] (with `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Helvetica` and `Arial`) is not used: fontconfig maps Helvetica and Arial to other faces than `sans-serif` (Nimbus Sans, Liberation Sans), so the top bar would set differently from Chat and the other components that declare the four-entry stack. Tests: `ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.spec.ts` (the computed font of `body`, the top bar's title and source and the open panel's heading and empty line under the compiled stylesheet) and `ui/e2e/tests/opens-on-chat.spec.ts` (the top bar's computed font in the running app, and Inter reported loaded by `document.fonts` with the top bar and the bell panel on it).
- **Focus:** one global `:focus-visible` ring, 2 px in `--sc-focus` (`:150`).
- **Status chip** (`ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/status-chip.component.ts:73`, since 2026-11-09): one chip for a state on every screen, a 13 px leading glyph and a sentence-case label at 12 px and weight 600 in a 12 px-radius pill. Its tone picks the pair and the glyph (`:7`): ok on the ok container with a check, warn on the warn container with a triangle, error on the error container with a crossed circle, info on the info container with an arc, neutral on `--sc-surface-2` under `--sc-muted` with a dot. The label comes from a map on the screen, so a raw state such as `crashed` never reaches the owner. AI Sources, the worker card and Chat connections use it; the other screens take it with their own migration ([[pages/roadmap/prototype-migration]]).
- **Notices** (since 2026-11-10): every notice, an error or a success included, is the artboards' inverse card, themed once on `.sc-notice` through Material's snack-bar tokens: `--sc-inverse-bg` under `--sc-inverse-fg`, the action in `--sc-inverse-action`, 10 px corners; the kind classes stay for the tests and the notification center, without colours of their own.
- **Dialog card** (since 2026-11-10): a dialog opened with `panelClass: 'sc-dialog'` takes the artboards' dialog card from the stylesheet, through Material's dialog tokens: 14 px corners, `--sc-surface`, a 1 px `--sc-line` ring and the `--sc-shadow` shadow (`ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss:198`); opened with `backdropClass: 'sc-dialog-backdrop'` it sits on `--sc-scrim` (`:208`) instead of the CDK's 32 % black. Settings' credentials modal, the voice model dialog and the shared confirmation use both. The shared confirmation (`confirmAction`, `ui/packages/renderer/src/app/core/confirm-dialog.component.ts:112`) is the card every artboard draws for a destructive confirmation: 440 px wide, 120 px from the top, 20 px padding, a 17 px title at 600, the body in `--sc-fg-2`, Cancel focused (the global focus-visible ring, no tint) and the action white on `--sc-danger` with its glyph, the trash unless the caller names another (the unplugged plug for every Disconnect), both 36 px.
- **Material buttons and badge** (`ui/packages/renderer/src/styles.scss:172`): the overrides are declared on `html` and on `.sc-dark-canvas`, so they resolve in the pinned scheme. Outlined: `--sc-fg` on `--sc-line`, 16 px side padding. Text: 10 px side padding, the accent label Material gives it. Filled: `--sc-on-accent` on `--sc-accent-strong` for every screen (since 2026-11-10), as every artboard draws its main action; the dark `--sc-accent-strong` stays #3466e8, because the artboards' #3b6ff2 under white is 4.43:1, under the 4.5:1 the stylesheet's contrast row requires. Badge: white 11 px bold on `--sc-danger`, 18 px, overlapping by 14 px. An `sc-icon` in a button label is set to the label's middle (`:228`).
