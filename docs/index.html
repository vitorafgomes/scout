The chatops assistant for Discord, Slack and Teams that reads first and writes only with your say-so.

Scout answers operational and business questions right in your team chat by reading from your cloud providers, source control and code repositories. Every write a feature performs is declared, granted by you and, when it cannot be undone, approved one action at a time.

What Scout is

Answers where your team already talks

Mention Scout in Discord, Slack or Teams and ask in plain language. Replies arrive in the same channel, in a thread, with citations.

Reads from the systems you run

Kubernetes clusters from your kubeconfig, GitHub repositories and the code of the projects you index. Pluggable providers for the model: Ollama on your machine by default, or ChatGPT, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI and OpenRouter.

Writes only with your grant

The first features are read-only. A feature that writes declares exactly what, you enable it in the desktop, and an action that cannot be undone waits for your approval there. Every write is logged.

How it works

Scout is two pieces that run on your machine. The desktop app is where you connect things. The worker is a .NET service that listens in your chat platform, reads from the systems you connected and composes the answer with the model you chose.

What leaves your machine is the chat traffic, the calls to the systems you connected (source control, cloud) and the calls to the model provider you picked. With Ollama, your prompts stay on your machine; the chat traffic and the calls to the systems you connected still leave it.

  1. 1
    Someone asks in chatA mention, a direct message or a voice message. Once you keep an allow-list, only the people on it get an answer.
  2. 2
    The worker readsKubernetes, GitHub and the other plugins you enabled answer with read-only calls. Voice is transcribed locally with Whisper.
  3. 3
    The model composes, Scout repliesStreamed into the channel, in a thread, with the sources it read. You can also ask from the floating Scout button on your desktop, without any chat platform.

Get started in three steps

1. Install the desktop app

Download the package for your system above and open it. Scout runs from the tray.

2. Connect a chat client and a model

Paste a Discord bot token, or create the Slack app from Scout's manifest and paste its two tokens; the app detects the rest. Pick Ollama on your machine or sign in to a provider.

3. Pair yourself and ask

In Settings, choose Pair from chat and send the six-digit code to Scout in a direct message. You are then on the allow-list. Mention Scout and ask.
